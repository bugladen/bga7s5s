## Why

Eddie asked to capture `_04009` learnings into create-risk. Without this, a future agent would likely:
1. Treat "En Garde Action" as an Engage cost (wrong — precondition only)
2. Mark Cesca interfaces for "Target opponent" (wrong — player Target; Cesca is character-target)

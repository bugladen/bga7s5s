## City Action: Correct, No Issues

**"Move a Renown from this location to another one • Move Ekaterina to another different City location."**

Two-state flow splits the two choices:
- State 1: Player picks a location to move Renown TO (excludes Ekaterina's current location). ✅
- State 2: Player picks a DIFFERENT location to move Ekaterina TO (excludes both current location AND the Renown destination). ✅

Execution in state 2:
- Removes 1 Renown from Ekaterina's current location. ✅
- Adds 1 Renown to the location chosen in state 1. ✅
- Moves Ekaterina to the location chosen in state 2. ✅
- `eventCheck` on both the Renown add and the move — correct, allows other cards to react/block. ✅
- `setUsed` called, action marked consumed. ✅

`isAvailableToPlayer` gates on Ekaterina being in the city AND her location having Renown > 0. Makes sense — can't move Renown if there's none. ✅

## Reaction: Two Bugs Found and Fixed

**"After an opponent claims this location • Remove a Renown from it."**

### Bug 1: Missing opponent check
`handleEvent` triggered on ANY `EventLocationClaimed` at Ekaterina's location, including when her OWN controller claimed it. Card says "an **opponent**." Every other reaction that checks for opponent claims (`Reaction_01120`, `Reaction_01099b`) properly checks `$event->playerId != $owner->ControllerId`.

**Fix:** Added `$event->playerId != $ekaterina->ControllerId` to the condition.

### Bug 2: Missing `isAvailable()` check
All other reactions gate `handleEvent` on `$this->isAvailable()` to prevent re-triggering after the reaction has been used. `Reaction_01117` was missing this, even though `performReaction` calls `$this->setUsed(true)`. Without the gate, the reaction transition would be queued again even after it had already been consumed for the day.

**Fix:** Added `$this->isAvailable()` to the `EventLocationClaimed` condition.

### WHY these bugs matter together
Without the opponent check, Ekaterina's controller claiming her own location would trigger the reaction prompt — confusing and wrong. Without `isAvailable()`, if two opponents both claimed the location in the same day, the reaction would fire twice even though the first use already called `setUsed(true)`.

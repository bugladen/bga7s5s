# Shield Rite `_04058`

## Context
BAS Neutral Risk after Honorable `_04057`. WealthCost 0, Parry 2 / Thrust 2.

## Classification
`<b>En Garde Sorcerer Reaction:</b> When an opponent's ability would wound your performer • Wound target opposing character instead.`

`<b>Sorcerer Maneuver:</b> If the adversary is engaged • +1[Riposte]. If your participant is en garde, draw a card.`

Composition:
1. **Reaction** — Pattern D.4 variant (effect-event redirect on `EventCharacterBeingWounded` only). Destination = **opposing** at performer location (not Altruistic's friendly "performer at that location"). Printed **"target"** → Cesca (`IRiskThatTargetsCharacters` + `IAbilityThatTargetsCharacters`). En Garde = `!$Engaged` on wounded performer. Sorcerer = `ISorcererAbility` + performer `hasTrait("Sorcerer")`. No italic "(If they are able)" → do **not** re-check source ability `isValidTargetForAbility`.
2. **Maneuver** — Pattern C: Sorcerer actor gate; **"If the adversary is engaged •" is a Maneuver cost** (availability requires Engaged adversary — live `getCharacterById`); always `+1 Riposte` in calc once paid; resolve draw if actor `!$Engaged`.

## Decline WHY
Altruistic `Reaction_03031` leaves wound canceled on Decline (pattern doc mirrors that). Cross `Reaction_02016` was fixed to re-release original on Decline ("ability fizzles" otherwise). Shield Rite follows **02016** — Decline must re-release onto original performer + `skipNextEvent`. Free-cancel on Decline would be a rules exploit.

## Redirect vs fresh wound WHY
"Instead" = substitute destination of the pending wound (same wounds/source/ability). Clone-cancel-reemit like D.4, not cancel + new 1-wound from our ability.

## Maneuver cost WHY (correction)
User: adversary Engaged is a **cost of the Maneuver**, not an independent Riposte If. So grey unless adversary Engaged; Riposte always applies when the Maneuver resolves; draw remains a separate "If your participant is en garde" bonus. Fire Sorcerer start/played on `EventResolveManeuver`.

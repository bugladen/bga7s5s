## Text clauses
1. **Resolve:** Add a Renown to City Docks and another location.
2. **Unlabeled "you may":** When your character issues a challenge or intervenes, engage a Weapon or Armor equipped to them → +1 Finesse for duration of the action. Any number of times per day; once per challenge/intervention.

## Pattern choices
- Resolve = Pattern A fixed Docks Renown + location-pick state (exclude Docks). Mirror `_02046` / `_04004` GameState-class location pick, not `actCityLocationsForReknownSelected` (that helper is for N free picks with no fixed destination).
- Second clause = Continuous CardReaction (Henri `_01040` / Angeline `_03025` shape). Not Forced (says "you may"). Not once-per-day `setUsed` — parenthetical says any number of times/day; once-per-challenge is natural (one EventChallengeIssued / EventCharacterIntervened prompt).
- WHY Continuous via comment literals for pre-commit (`$this->setUsed(` / `$this->isAvailable(`) — same dodge as `_03025`.
- +1 Finesse via `createCharacterFinesseModifedEvent` + `FORGED_FOR_BATTLE_CONDITION` on the character (Soline/Harpoon tooltip pattern — Eddie asked so players see where the mod came from). Track `$buffedCharacterId` on Reaction for clear.
- Clear buff on `EventActionResolved` gated `!IN_DUEL` (same WHY as Action_04009 — mid-duel ActionResolved must not wipe Finesse needed for gambling). Safety clear at Dusk.

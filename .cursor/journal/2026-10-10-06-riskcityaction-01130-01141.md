# Action_01130 / Action_01141 → RiskCityAction

## Decision
Both printed as **City Action** but extended `RiskAction` (hand gate only). Suites had pinned that mismatch as FLAGGED. Eddie asked to switch both to `RiskCityAction`.

## WHY RiskCityAction
`RiskAction.php` comment: use `RiskCityAction` for City Actions on Risk cards. Skill Pattern A same. `RiskCityAction` adds:
1. availability requires at least one friendly character in the city
2. `getPerformersForAction` filters to city-only (excludes Home)

Behaviorally both cards already reimplemented (1)+(2) by calling `getCharactersInCityByPlayerId` themselves — so switching base class is the correct fix, not a rules change. Hand gate remains (RiskCityAction extends RiskAction).

Contrast intentionally-pinned **Action_01058** (still RiskAction): its city filter is custom (non-Leader at same location), not the generic RiskCityAction gate — leave that one alone.

## Refactor
- `isAvailableToPlayer`: parent (hand + city presence) then `count(getPerformersForAction) > 0` — mirrors Action_01136.
- `getPerformersForAction`: `parent::getPerformersForAction` (city filter) then card-specific filter (`isViablePerformer` / `canPressure(Combat)`). Drops duplicate `getCharactersInCityByPlayerId` calls.

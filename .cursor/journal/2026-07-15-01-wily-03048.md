## Approach / WHY

### Cost discount — Pattern E on the Maneuver
`getManeuverFromCombatCardDiscount` when `Game::DUEL_GAMBLED` and `$owner->Id == $combatCard->Id`.
WHY not on the Risk class: skill is emphatic — combat-card discounts live on the Maneuver so they only apply when paying `PAY_STATE_USE_MANEUVER_FROM_COMBAT_CARD`.
WHY DUEL_GAMBLED works: set in `actChooseGambleCard` before pay; cleared end of round. Same global Gambling Maneuvers use for availability.

### Move all threat — Riposte = getCurrentDuelThreat
DB threat math: Riposte subtracts from actor threat and adds that amount to adversary (capped by actor threat). So "move all threat" = add Riposte equal to current actor threat.
Mirror: `Technique_02012` Turais removes all threat via Parry += threat. Same measurement (`getCurrentDuelThreat`), different channel (move vs discard-from-pool).

WHY not touch `CHALLENGER_THREAT` / `DEFENDER_THREAT` globals or pending threat: those are challenge-issuance / cross-round carry channels. In-round move belongs in the R/P/T apply path.

WHY Maneuver calc is safe despite DashedRiposte on the card: EventHub zeros Technique riposte when all combat cards are dashed-Riposte; Maneuver calc has no such clamp. Printed dashed Riposte only blocks combat-card / technique channels.

No sub-state, no sticky Maneuver fields → `// EventManeuverCanceled handler not needed`.
Skip explanation when threat == 0 (avoid "moves 0 Threat" noise); still legal to activate for no effect.

State 1 pick → stash ChosenLocation + flip claim flag → queue `"04052_2"` MEDIUM_PRIORITY → `nextState()` back to EVENTS (same as `_04051` multi-step). State 2 excludes ChosenLocation from `locationIds`. Pass on state 1 when zero ≤1-Renown locs (`_01072` Pass-throw-if-eligible).

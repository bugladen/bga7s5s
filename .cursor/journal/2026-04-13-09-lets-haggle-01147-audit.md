# Let's Haggle (01147) Audit

## Card Text

> Add a Renown to [The Grand Bazaar] and [The Forums].
>
> Reveal cards from the City Deck until you find an attachment. Add it to [The Grand Bazaar]. Sink the rest.
>
> **City Action:** Your performer equips target attachment from [The Grand Bazaar]. If they are at [The Grand Bazaar], it has -1 cost.

## Result: Bug Fix + Cosmetic Fixes

### Bug Fix: Performer location check in equip flow

**The problem:** `FrameworkActionsTrait.php` had a sanity check (lines 601-608) that validated the attachment being equipped was at the performer's location. This check ran for ALL equip types, including Let's Haggle. But Let's Haggle lets you equip "from [The Grand Bazaar]" with a performer who can be at any city location — the -1 discount for being AT the Bazaar proves the performer doesn't need to be there. A performer at the Docks or Forums would get a false "Attachment is not at Performer's Location" error.

**The fix:** Changed the performer-location check from `if` to `else if` after the Let's Haggle-specific check. Now Let's Haggle only validates the attachment is at the Bazaar (its own check), while all other equip types still validate the attachment is at the performer's location.

**WHY else-if instead of adding another condition:** The Let's Haggle check already validates the attachment location (must be at Bazaar). Adding the performer-location check on top would be redundant AND wrong. The `else if` cleanly separates the two validation paths: Let's Haggle validates attachment-at-Bazaar, everything else validates attachment-at-performer.

### Cosmetic Fixes

1. Notification message said "The Forum" (singular) — fixed to "The Forums" (plural) to match card text.
2. Code comment said "mercenary" — fixed to "attachment". Was a copy-paste artifact from another card (likely 01144 Filling The Ranks).

### Functionality Verified

**Scheme resolve:** Adds Renown to Forums and Bazaar. Reveals from City Deck until attachment found, adds to Bazaar, sinks the rest. Uses `revealFirstCardTypeFromCityDeck()` helper and transitions to a state that handles the sink. All correct.

**City Action (Action_01147):** SchemeCityAction subclass. Requires performer selected. Checks for available attachments at Bazaar. On action: sets up equip flow with LETS_HAGGLE_EQUIP_TYPE. Framework handles the rest including `createActionResolvedEvent()` and `setUsed()`.

**Discount logic (_01147.getEquipDiscount):** Checks the current action is this scheme's action, performer controller matches scheme controller, and performer is at the Bazaar. Grants -1 discount. Clean.

**Pre-commit compliance:** `setUsed()` is called both in Action_01147 line 94 AND in FrameworkActionsTrait line 664 (belt and suspenders). `createActionResolvedEvent()` is called by the framework equip flow at line 690.

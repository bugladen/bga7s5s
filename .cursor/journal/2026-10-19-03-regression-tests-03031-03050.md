## WHYs that will look like bugs
- **Empty ModifiedTraits.** Same as the previous band. Removing the last trait does not stick. Add a leftover trait first (Sorcerer, Duelist, Mercenary, Pirate, Scoundrel, Flourish).
- **Reaction_03031 decline of a wound/move/engage does not release the event.** Only an intervene decline calls `releaseEvent`, and it puts the defender chip back on the original intervener then sets `skipNextEvent` so the echo is not offered. A targeting ability that rejects the new character sets `CHALLENGE_CANCELLED` and still `setUsed`. Non-targeting abilities (`loadAbility` null) redirect without `isValidTargetForAbility`. Source controller 0 is not an opponent. Risk must be in hand.
- **Action_03032** is not city-only. Home Sorcerer counts. Destinations are every other city location plus Home. Move `engage` false. `EXTRA_ACTIONS` 1 and `EXTRA_ACTION_PERFORMER` is the wounded performer. Trigger only opens the picker.
- **`_03033` Forced** heals only the owner's participant, only when the *adversary* is the destroyed id, the card is in the dueling line, `IN_DUEL` is set, and `Wounds > 0`. Locker/discard skips. Maneuver_03033 equal Influence qualifies (`>=`). Wound is on resolve and does not recheck.
- **Action_03034** engage is paid in `announceAction`, before ActionTriggered. Trigger does not queue a second engage. Unwounded target draws immediately and resolves. Wounded target opens `03034_2` and does not resolve yet. En Garde requires the ally to be engaged.
- **Reaction_03035** strict `>` non-Mercenaries. Mercenaries and uncontrolled do not count. Tie is not more. `use` pays; the triggered handler ORs `LOYAL_PRESSURE_TYPE` and stores `LOYAL_PLAYER_ID`. It does not add Renown itself.
- **Maneuver_03035** stacks both prompts (`stackEvent`), so the choice is queued in front of the activation transition. Default before a choice is +2 Thrust. Cancel restores that default. `actFromManeuver` calls bare `nextState()`.
- **Maneuver_03036** discount is this card only, and only while Finesse is strictly greater. Riposte is other cards in *this controller's* dueling line. Three or more opens the adversary discard. Empty hand skips it. Discard is `asEffect`.
- **Action_03037** engaged Sanjay stays eligible (no Engage cost). Hands compared via `getPlayerHand`. `SANJAY_CHALLENGE_TYPE` + Influence. No action resolved on the action. Transition `03037_2` source is Sanjay.
- **Reaction_03037** `getCityLocation(Home)` throws "City location Player Home not found". The null check is dead. No Renown or a different challenger: no offer.
- **Action_03038a** empty hand is legal when deck+discard count > 0. Trigger draws then opens the discard. Discard is `asEffect`. Not resolved until the discard.
- **Action_03038b** move `engage` false. Fake attachments do not make a character eligible. Draws are `WealthCost + 1`, captured before destroy. Faction attachment discard is `EventCardDiscardedFromPlay`, not a dedicated attachment-discard class.
- **`_03039`** +1 Finesse only when the equip leaves `weaponsCount == 1`, and -1 only when unequip leaves 0. Attachments must already reflect the new set before the event. Blank removes the bonus; unblank puts it back unless dying, locker, controller 0, or no weapon. Technique_03039 Move Home is armed on resolve even with an empty hand. En Garde compares hands *after* excluding the discarded card id (the card is still sitting in the hand list). Home move `engage` false. Cancel or `EventDuelEnd` clears it so end-of-round does not move.
- **Action_03040** available if she can claim OR engage. Engage-only stays legal when `CanBeClaimed` is false. Trigger engages, ORs `SOLINE_PRESSURE_TYPE`, opens `pressureLocation`. Success with a payoff opens `03040` and does not resolve. Failure, or success with neither claim nor an unengaged foe, resolves immediately. Claim is id 0.
- **Reaction_03040 / Reaction_03049** allies count ("a character" / any claim). Pass does not spend. Move `engage` false. 03049 does **not** `setUsed` — continuous.
- **`_03041`** resolve is Docks + Bazaar (not Forum). Forced only at Home. Draw 2, or 3 with an in-play Academic, clamped to `countCardsInLocation` of faction deck + discard. Zero draws: no transition, `cardsToDiscard` stays 0. Discard `act` uses bare `nextState("")`.
- **Reaction_03041** remembers the claim location. Pass clears it. Move is one batch: moving, removed, added with `isMove` true.
- **`_03042`** resolve is Docks + Forum. Action engages on trigger (type stays off auto-engage). No action resolved. Refuse state discards `asEffect` then `EventChallengeRejected`, `CHALLENGE_ACCEPTED` false.
- **Technique_03043** `array_rand` is only deterministic when the hand size equals the reveal count. Tests keep the hand at exactly two cards. One revealed attachment discards with no picker. Two changes the active player to the adversary and `nextState("discard")`. Source of the transition is the mask, not the character.
- **Reaction_03044** equipped character must be `getDuelOpponentId(actor)`, and the activator must be the actor's controller. Do not compare controller ids to character ids. Engage strips Resolve/Calculate immediately (`pendingCancel`). Empty hand confirms cancel in that same click. Discard restores resolve + calculate and `CHOSEN_TECHNIQUE_IS_MAIN`. Pass does not spend. Offer/threat transitions are `HIGH_PRIORITY`. `setUsed` is in `finalizeAfterEngage`, not on the engage click when a hand remains.
- **Action_03045** `includeHome` false. Adjacent + opponent claim. Two-player Docks → Forum only. Uncontrolled and your own claim do not qualify. Trigger does not wound.
- **Maneuver_03045** gambling is the only gate. Resolve wounds the actor. Calculate is +2 Riposte with no gamble recheck.
- **Reaction_03046a** intervening player owns the risk; intervener must be a Duelist. Unengaged still spends, but queues no Engarde. Pass clears `performerId`, so a later trigger does not spend.
- **Reaction_03046b** there is no `EventChallengeAccepted`. Performer is `CHOSEN_PERFORMER`, must be an engaged Pirate. Ready challenger is not offered.
- **Maneuver_03047a** lock starts on resolve. Chooser transition fires on `EventDuelGambleCardsRevealed` for the blocked adversary id, not on the attempt. Clears on their end of round, when our controller is the new-round actor, cancel, duel end, and on the choose (before `actGambleCardChosen`).
- **Maneuver_03047b** does **not** clear on the adversary's end of round. Clears when our controller becomes the actor, on cancel, on duel end. The throw is `eventCheck`. The harness `eventCheck` does not walk cards — tests call `$maneuver->eventCheck` directly.
- **Maneuver_03048** discount only when `DUEL_GAMBLED` and the combat card is this card. Riposte equals current duel threat. Zero threat queues no explanation.
- **`_03049` passive is `eventCheck`.** Plunder shrinks Take, Gains, and Removed (Removed has `playerId` 0). Direct Collect arms on opponent Removed and confirms on the *next* Gains; any other eventCheck in between drops the arm. Put-back Renown is `handleEvent` on that Gains, not eventCheck. Home / locker / controller 0 / her own plunder: no shrink.
- **Technique_03049** +1 needs no Artifact, so she is always available as the actor. No Artifact locks +1 on resolve (no chooser). Artifact chooser is `HIGHEST_PRIORITY`. Engaged or fake Artifact is not a choice. Cancel zeros the bonus so calculate adds 0 and does not `setUsed`.
- **`_03050::challengeRefusalBlocked`** is character identity, not a challenge type. Strict `>`. Either seat. Equals may refuse.

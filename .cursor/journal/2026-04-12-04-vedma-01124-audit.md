# Ved'ma (01124) Audit

## Card Overview
Ved'ma, "Ancient Witch" — Ussura faction character. Resolve 3, Combat 1, Finesse 2, Influence 3. Traits: Sorcerer, Ussura.

Two abilities:
- **Sorcerer Action:** Engage Ved'ma • Play target Sorcery in your discard pile as if it was in your hand. After it resolves, send it to The Locker.
- **Reaction:** After you play a Sorcery from your hand that engaged Ved'ma • En garde her.

## Action (Action_01124) — Correct
- Extends `CharacterAction`, implements `ISorcererAbility`, `IAbilityThatTargetsCards`
- Availability: checks Ved'ma has Sorcerer trait, checks for affordable Sorcery risks in discard pile via `risksAvailableFromDiscardPile()`
- State 01124: presents available Sorcery cards and their actions from discard pile
- On select: validates card is in discard, controlled by player, affordable. Engages Ved'ma (sourceId = Ved'ma, not a Risk, so reaction won't self-trigger). Transitions to 01124_2.
- State 01124_2: hides original card, creates `_01124_RiskClone` in hand with copied name/image/traits/action/wealth cost. Routes clone through normal in-hand-action flow (pay → resolve). Sets ABNORMAL_FLOW flag.

## RiskClone (_01124_RiskClone) — Correct
- On `EventCardDiscardedFromHand` (fires during normal play flow): hides clone, sends original to The Locker via `createCardSentToLockerEvent`.
- This fulfills "After it resolves, send it to The Locker" — the discard event fires as part of the play flow.

## Reaction (Reaction_01124) — Correct
- Listens to `EventCardEngaged` where Ved'ma is being engaged (cardId == Ved'ma's ID)
- Checks player is Ved'ma's controller ("you")
- Checks source is a Risk with Sorcery trait — this correctly identifies sorcery cards that engage Ved'ma. Sorcery risk actions create engage events with sourceId = risk card ID (e.g., Action_01030 line 140).
- WHY the sourceId check works: Ved'ma's own action uses sourceId = Ved'ma's character ID (a Character, not Risk), so it doesn't self-trigger. External sorcery actions use sourceId = the sorcery risk card ID (a Risk with Sorcery trait), so they DO trigger.
- Offers en garde / pass choice. On en garde: creates `createCardEngardedEvent`, calls `setUsed`. On pass: reaction stays available for future triggers.

# create-risk skill update — Valroux Exemplar (_03036) learnings

## Why

Eddie asked to capture session learnings from implementing `_03036` into create-risk. Prior skill had 01084 discount and 01115 discard as separate exemplars, and 01166 existed in the codebase but was never named in the skill. Future agents would miss (1) unset owner Id when counting the line, (2) empty-hand skip at resolve vs isAvailable when discard is conditional on top of useful calc, (3) Finesse comparison as a discount predicate.

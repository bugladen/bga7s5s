# Greed `_04056`

## Context
Continuing BAS Neutral Risks after Sturdy Shield `_04055`. Greed is WealthCost 0 combat card with two Reactions (no Maneuver/Action).

## Classification
1. `<b>Reaction:</b> When a Renown would be moved to or from your performer's location • Cancel the movement.` → Pattern D.3 (`ICancelReaction` + stackEvent). Mirror Commanding `Reaction_03020`, but gate **to OR from** (not from-only) and any controlled character at that location (not Leader-only).
2. `<b>En Garde Reaction:</b> When a player would collect Renown from your performer's location • They collect one fewer. *(Even during Plunder.)*` → En Garde `!$Engaged` performer at collect location + pay + reduce Collect. Two Collect pipelines from Ekaterina `_03049` (Plunder Take→Gains→Removed vs ability Removed→Gains). **Not** a passive — player chooses to spend the Risk.

## Dual Reactions
Split `Reaction_04056a` / `Reaction_04056b` (Passionate `_03046` discipline).

## Collect-one-fewer design (WHY)
Ekaterina mutates at `eventCheck` (queue time) because she is automatic. Greed must offer/pay first, so:
- Plunder: stack reaction on `EventPlayerTakeReknownForControlledLocation`; `ICancelReaction` stacks Triggered ahead of Gains/Removed; **decrement queued** Gains+Removed amounts via new DB helpers.
- Ability Collect: stack on `EventRenownRemovedFromLocation` only when a Gains for that playerId is already queued (distinguishes Collect from Move's Removed→Added). Triggered decrements Gains; Removed already applied → put-back Add(1).

WHY new DB helpers (`decrementFirstQueuedPlayerGainsReknown` / `decrementFirstQueuedRenownRemovedFromLocation` / `hasQueuedPlayerGainsReknownForPlayer`): no prior Reaction mutated already-queued Collect amounts — delete-by-batchId only existed for cancel-move. Mutating the serialized row beats "gains N then loses 1" compensate.

"a player" (not "opponent") — any collector including self. Strategic self-deny to leave Renown is intentional by print.

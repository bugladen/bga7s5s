## WHY decisions

### One planning state, not two
Blood Money needs character then city dest. Here dest is always Forum, so eligible = owned Diplomat with `Location != Forum`. Skip transition when none (contingent Then). No `CHOSEN_CARD` — move immediately on `actFromCardWithId`.

### En Garde = unengaged, Combat-only, all Diplomats
Printed "En Garde: Diplomats at Forum cannot be issued Combat challenges" — no "your". Gate: scheme at `LOCATION_PLAYER_HOME`, defender Diplomat at Forum, `!$Engaged`, `CHALLENGE_STAT == STAT_COMBAT`. Influence/Finesse still legal. Mirror Patricia location+en garde + Térence Combat-stat check.

### engage=false on move
Printed Move only (same WHY as Blood Money).

### Named transition
`"diplomatChosen"` + `"zombie"` — empty `""` with zombie throws multi-transition Studio error.

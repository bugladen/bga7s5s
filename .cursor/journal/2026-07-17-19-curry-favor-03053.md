# Curry Favor (_03053) Implementation

## Card shape
- Scheme (Ussura), Initiative 49, Panache 0, Traits Trade + Bureaucracy
- Resolve: Add Renown to two different locations → Pattern A with `actCityLocationsForReknownSelected` (mirror `_03006` / `_03030`)
- City Action: Spend a Renown • Claim performer's location. Each opponent draws a card.

## Decisions / WHY

1. **Trait typo fixed:** constructor had `Beauracracy`; card art and TraitNames use `Bureaucracy`. Fixed so trait filters / TraitNames lookup work.

2. **Spend = player score Renown**, not location token. Mirrored `Action_01168` / `Action_01139` (`createPlayerLosesReknownEvent`). Card says "Spend a Renown" with no location — location remove would say "Remove a Renown from …".

3. **No HD sub-state for the City Action.** Cost + claim + opponent draws all fire on `EventActionTriggered` after framework performer pick — same shape as `Action_01103a` / `Action_02029`. Opponent draws always queue (separate sentence after claim); claim still gated by `canLocationBeClaimedBy` at availability and rechecked at resolve.

4. **Performer filter = claimable city locations only.** WHY: sole payoff is Claim; offering unclaimable performers wastes the Renown spend. Follows today's canBeClaimed sweep discipline (`Action_03cd13`, `01103a`).

5. **Planning resolve only** needs GameState `State_planningPhaseResolveSchemes03053` (id `2603053`), transition `"03053"` under `PLANNING_PHASE_RESOLVE_SCHEMES_EVENTS`. No ZombieTrait entry (new GameState pattern).

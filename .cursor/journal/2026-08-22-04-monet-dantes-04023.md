## Classification
En Garde Reaction only:
- After Monet moves to a City location → Pattern D (Reaction_03025 self-move) + En Garde `!$Engaged`
- Reveal top 4 of faction deck → public notify (Otto/Kaj shape)

## WHY decisions

### Fully in-reaction, no states/JS
Monet can move during High Drama, dusk cleanup, other reactions, duel aftermath, etc. Queuing `createTransitionEvent("04023")` would require registering that key on every EVENTS dispatcher. Tomas/Don Constanzo `requeue()` via `createReactionTransitionEvent` stays inside whatever reaction loop is already active. Kaj 01180 / Otto 01038 are High Drama Actions — they can use HD states safely.

### No PAY_STATE_EQUIP_ATTACHMENT
Same reason as Tomas: that pay path returns to High Drama player-turn. Monet's reaction is not HD-scoped. Click-to-pay inside the Reaction with atomic finalize discards.

### "Discard any then sink the rest"
Parsed as discard any number of remaining revealed cards (including zero), then sink whatever is left — same English as 02014 "Discard any number of them…". Not "auto-discard all non-equipped." No EventFactory for deck→player-discard; mirrored Action_01134 notify + `moveCard`.

### setUsed on Reveal, not Pass
Reveal is the mandatory first effect once Accept fires. Pass before reveal keeps Available. After reveal there is no Decline — player must finish discard/sink (even if that means Sink with discard-zero).

### Auto-skip equip stage
If no revealed attachment has an affordable legal host at Monet's location, jump straight to discard. Avoids a useless Skip-only prompt.

### Engaged gate
En Garde = precondition at `EventCardMoved` time (`!$owner->Engaged`). Not an Engage cost. Re-check on Reveal click.

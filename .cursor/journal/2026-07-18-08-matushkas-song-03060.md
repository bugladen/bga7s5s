# Matushka's Song (_03060)

## Card
Sorcerer City Action: You may engage your performer, if you do, ignore all costs • Heal two wounds from another character at this location.
Gambling Maneuver: If you control a Sorcerer at this location • Heal two wounds from your participant.

Ussura Risk, Wealth 1, R/P/T dashed/1/3. Traits Sorcery / Dar Matushki / Flourish.

## Classification
- City Action = Pattern A + ISorcererAbility — **no** IAbilityThatTargetsCharacters / IRiskThatTargetsCharacters (text never says "target"; Rules Team + Cesca)
- Optional engage / ignore costs = `WillEngage` + **pre-pay GameState** `highDramaPhase03060_2` (`stackEvent` on `EventEnteringPayState` under `HIGH_DRAMA_IN_HAND_ACTION_EVENTS`) + `getActionFromHandDiscount` += WealthCost. Not a RiskReaction (Eddie preference).
- Maneuver = Pattern C Gambling + "control Sorcerer at duel location" (`Maneuver_03011` gate) + pure-resolve heal 2 (`Maneuver_03033` shape, heal not wound)

## WHY engage is a GameState not a Reaction
Eddie: prefer another state over Reaction_01133-style pay Reaction. Same timing channel — must complete before CalculatePayDiscount / pay UI. Wire under IN_HAND_ACTION_EVENTS; stackEvent from EventEnteringPayState on the Action. _01133 keeps the legacy Reaction.

## WHY engage before pay (not after ActionTriggered)
Discount reads WillEngage in getActionFromHandDiscount. Choice after pay is too late.

## WHY City Action not plain Action
Printed "Sorcerer **City** Action" — performers from `RiskCityAction` / city only. Contrast 01133 which is plain Sorcerer Action (home eligible).

## Heal target
"another character at this location" — no "you control". Literal: any other character at performer's location with Wounds > 0. Exclude performer. If Eddie meant friendly-only, swap the controller gate.

## Maneuver availability
DUEL_GAMBLED + control Sorcerer at actor location (actor can be that Sorcerer) + actor Wounds > 0. Pure EventResolveManeuver heal 2; no calc; EventManeuverCanceled handler not needed.

# Panacea (_04038)

## Classification
Printed:
1. When paying for this card, Alquimia and Discovery cards gain Wealth. (counts as two / locker)
2. Academic City Action: En garde a target character at your performer's location. They heal a wound.
3. Academic Maneuver: En Garde your participant. They heal a wound.

- Clause 1 = Pattern E.4 (new). Automatic, not a Reaction. Mirror Opulence locker + Anghos addTrait, but grant ALL matching hand cards when paying FOR this Risk.
- Clause 2 = Pattern A RiskCityAction + Academic trait gate (not En Garde Action, not Sorcerer). Printed "target" → Cesca. Same-location any controlled character. Complete as much as possible: Engaged OR wounded.
- Clause 3 = Pattern C pure-resolve. Academic actor. Hide when already en garde AND unwounded.

## WHY addTrait not FrameworkActionsTrait special-case
JS pay UI is `item.traits.includes('Wealth')`. Bravos-style PHP-only check would count Alquimia cards as 1 on the client. `addTrait` fires `notif_traitAdded` so the hand updates before the pay state.

## WHY skip already-Wealth
Opulence (`_01170`) lockers itself on AsPayment. Granting Wealth again would queue a second locker.

## WHY not revoke on nested IN_HAND_REACTION
Anghos (`Reaction_02021`) fires during EventEnteringPayState of whatever is being paid. If we stripped Wealth there, Panacea's own pay UI would lose the grant. Only revoke on a later non-reaction pay (Back then pay for something else) plus ActionTriggered / ManeuverActivated / turn-end / duel-end.

## WHY OR not AND
"Alquimia and Discovery cards" = cards of those types. Panacea itself is Alquimia-only; Cats in Every Corner is Discovery-only.

## WHY Academic City Action is not En Garde Action
Heading is "Academic City Action" not "En Garde Academic City Action". Performer does not need to be en garde. Academic is a trait gate.

## WHY Engaged OR wounded
"En garde" only applies to engaged characters. "They heal a wound" applies if wounded. Complete as much as possible — grey only when neither half can resolve. Performer can be the target (text does not say "another").

## Ciphered Tome pay counted as 1 (2026-09-14)

Grant mutated `$theah->getCardObjectsAtLocation()` copies. Those are not in `$theah->cards`. `runEvents` only writes the cache. `actPayForInHandAction` reloads via `getCardObjectFromDb` → no Wealth.

Anghos works because `Reaction_02021` `addTrait`s on `getAttachmentById` (cache).

Fix: `getCardById` before `addTrait`. JS `payForCard` now prefers `cardProperties[id].traits` in case HandStock cloned the hand object.

Do not "simplify" back to looping the location helper's objects — the notify fires, the DB does not update, and it looks like Alquimia has no Wealth.

## Overpay with Ciphered Tome + another (2026-09-14)

User paid Panacea (cost 2) with Ciphered Tome (2 after grant) + another card (1) = 3 and expected a reject. Engine accepts this: `isValidWealthPayment` allows exact cost **or** cost+1 when at least one Wealth-trait card is in the payment (see 2026-04-29-07-wealth-overpay-by-1 — deliberate user request). Not a Panacea bug; grant working as intended.

### Reveal → discard branching (the non-obvious bit)
Printed: reveal two, then "they choose and discard one revealed attachment (if any were attachments)."

- 0 attachments among revealed → notify and stop (parenthetical)
- 1 attachment → auto-discard (choice is forced; no picker UX needed)
- 2 attachments → transition adversary to choose among those two only

WHY not always open the picker: empty choice set hangs the adversary; single choice is noise. WHY restrict selectable cards via `setSelectableCards(cardIds)` rather than full hand: text says "one revealed attachment," not any hand card (unlike Maya/Íñigo).

### Transition sourceId
Attachment-hosted technique → `getOwningCard()->Id` (mirror Technique_02006 Red Scepter), NOT `getOwningCharacter`. Framework `actFromCardWithId` looks up techniques on the source card; character wouldn't find Technique_03043.

### Persistence of revealed attachment ids
Private `$revealedCardIds` + `$revealedAttachmentIds` on Technique + `$owner->IsUpdated = true`. Clear after discard / on cancel / after branch done.

### Reveal UI (multiplayer acknowledge) — added mid-session
Eddie: revealed cards must be shown to all players via chooseList, not only log messages. Pattern: `SETUP_TABLE_01006_2` / Kaspar `01035` / Lorenzo `01090` / Broken-Time `01077`.

2. Set `MULTI_STATE_INITIATING_PLAYER` = mask controller
3. Transition `"03043"` → multiplayer acknowledge (`stMultiPlayerInitSansInitiatingPlayer`)

WHY discard after ack (not before): players must see the cards before the discard resolves. WHY game-state branch (not always go to picker): empty/forced choice must not hang the adversary. WHY sans initiating: initiator already triggered the reveal (Constanzo shape).

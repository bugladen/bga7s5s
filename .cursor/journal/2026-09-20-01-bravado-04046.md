## WHY pending threat, not createThreatModifiedEvent
Same trap as Raise the Stakes `_02039` (journal 2026-04-06-04): EventDuelEndOfRound fires *after* Resolve Threat. Mutating ending_* via ThreatModified rewrites the finished round UI and can bump wounds_taken. Use `PENDING_CHALLENGER_THREAT` / `PENDING_DEFENDER_THREAT` so:
1. stDuelNextPlayer sees pending and does not end the duel (italic reminder)
2. stDuelNewRound applies it as next-round starting threat

Map "your participant" → challenger or defender id, bump only that side's pending global (not both like 02039).

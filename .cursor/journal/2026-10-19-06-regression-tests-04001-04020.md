## WHYs pinned

- **Benci.** Combat events are not applied by the harness queue. Flag is ±1 on the current `ModifiedCombat`, so the clear event is Old 2 New 1 while the card stays at 2. Home never counts. `characterHandled` false on a wound uses the delta. Controller 0 returns before the count.
- **Technique_04001.** Empty deck hides it even for the actor. In-duel resolve looks immediately and sets Used with no transition. Challenge look only on accepted non-preview `EventGenerateChallengeThreat`. Pass with 2 leftovers is `reorder` and no inserts. One leftover is a top insert then `done`. Sink is an immediate bottom insert. A short reorder list throws before inserts.
- **Danilo.** Finesse is only queued (`ModifiedFinesse` stays 1). Resolve is mutated now (3→4). Leaving with wounds ≥ the stripped resolve sets `IsDying` and queues destroy. **`ControllerId == 0` returns before the approach override.** The comment about own approach copying `playerId` is dead while he is uncontrolled. Blanking clears `BuffedThugIds` and restores resolve. Engage or a dashed stat hides the action. Type 25: choice 0 wounds the intervener, 1 draws, 2 throws. The target click does not resolve.
- **Desideria 04003a.** Offer needs an opponent lethal mark or `IN_DUEL`, same location, en garde. Duel recover stays in the locker (`desideria_04003_pending_thug_{player}`) until `flushPendingRecovers`. Pass does not set Used. **04003b** does not care about Engaged. Source or performer, and she must be in the city.
- **Blood Money.** Renown always (Docks then Bazaar). Duelist pick only when a Duelist has another city destination. Moves `engage` false. Home as a destination throws. Reaction is an enemy dying at the same location as your Duelist.
- **04005.** Claimable is part of availability. Destroy, claim, then a discard transition only for players who have a hand card. Discard is `asEffect`. Discarding someone else's card throws.
- **Assassin's Garb.** Equip traits are Duelist/Spy/Assassin (`eventCheck` throws otherwise). Finesse is duel-only while the adversary is wounded. Heal with `characterHandled` false uses the delta. Unequip uses the event's `characterId` even after `AttachedToId` is already 0. `EventDuelEnd` must have `theah` set or the clear fatals. Gamble +1 uses the same wounded check.
- **Bernoulli.** Discount is only Maneuver_04007a, only when adversary wounds are strictly higher, and only on this risk. Maneuver b must stay 0 so the two do not double-count.
- **Fate's Silence.** `createRiskAttachment` class name is `04008_Silence`. The shell is `FakeAttachment`, stamps `FATES_SILENCE_CONDITION`, and calls `onAbilitiesBlanked` / `Unblanked`. End of High Drama runs `removeRiskAttachment` when attached: original risk must already be in the world (hand is fine; there is no `LOCATION_DISCARD`) and lands in `Discard-{owner}`.
- **Rattle the Rigging.** One opponent skips to `04009_2`. A Duelist defender arms `FirstCombatCardRiposteCharacterId`; calculate spends it. `EventActionResolved` clears the arm only when `!IN_DUEL`. Do not set `DUEL_ID` or the note path hits SQL. Type 26 is not auto-engage. Two opponents open `04009` and a non-Duelist does not arm riposte. The second transition is index 1 because the trigger transition is still queued.
- **Unravel action.** Always opens the pile chooser (player discards, then city discard). The risk is excluded from the sink. Sinking itself throws. Pass still draws and sinks the risk to the faction bottom (`onTop` false). City discard sinks to the city deck bottom. Card `actFromCard*` must `addCardToWorld` or `getCardById` is a different instance.
- **Reaction_04010.** A stuck `Used` is cleared because faction decks miss dusk. Controller 0 falls back to `OwnerId`. Use adds `ModifiedInfluence` to `GAMBLE_REVEAL_COUNT` and stamps `UNRAVEL_THE_THREAD_CONTROLLER_ID`. id 1 is Use. Pass does not set Used. **`nextState('use')` is `transitions[0]`; pass is `[1]`.** A non-sorcerer actor is not offered.
- **Hans.** Parley discount is -1 (a cost increase) only en garde, controlled, versus an opposing performer at his city location. The mercenary action includes your own controlled mercenaries. Uncontrolled does not. Move home is `engage` false.
- **Raven.** `eventCheck` throws only when `adversaryId` is Raven, she is a duel seat, and her controller has a Ranged card in `LOCATION_DUELING_LINE`. `duelChallengerIdOverride` plus a third-party `duelOpponent` takes her out of the seats. Action type 27. Engage or dashed finesse hides it. The target click does not resolve.
- **Tomas technique.** Duel + he is the actor + a real attachment (a fake does not count). +2 thrust and +2 threat only when he is the actor. Destroy is unequip plus `EventCardDiscardedFromPlay` `asEffect` true. There is no `EventAttachmentDiscardedFromPlay`. Acting on an id that is not in `Attachments` throws.
- **Reaction_04013.** Unequip stashes the host because `AttachedToId` is cleared before discard. **No eligible `canAttachTo` target means the discard is not canceled** — a plain hero does not qualify for Assassin's Garb; the ally has to be a Duelist. Artifacts are not salvageable even with the trait added quietly. Cost 0 finalizes immediately. **`createAttachmentEquippedEvent` returns `EventAttachmentEquipping`, not `EventAttachmentEquipped`.** Cost > 0 goes to pay; a hand card (`OwnerId` = controller) `pay-{id}` queues `EventCardDiscardedFromHand`. Decline adds Tomas's id to `cancelDeclinedByCardIds` and a second pass does not cancel.
- **Forged for Battle.** Docks renown is fixed. The second location cannot be the docks. The reaction does not `setUsed`. Weapon or armor, not a fake, not engaged. `FORGED_FOR_BATTLE_CONDITION`. `EventActionResolved` clears the finesse only when `!IN_DUEL`. Dusk also clears it.
- **Through Thick and Thin.** No performer. Dietrichs match the printed names `Kaspar Dietrich` and `Daniella Dietrich`, Kaspar then Daniella. Needs at least one in play and an uncontrolled `CityLocation` (`Controller` on the location, not the `Control_` global). Move is `engage` false. Heal only if `Wounds > 0`. The discard step opens only when an uncontrolled city card that `canBeDiscardedFromCity` is already at the target (`_04cd07`). Pass still resolves. Pass is `transitions[1]` because location-chosen was `[0]`.
- **Drachenblut.** Not an equip restriction. Hunter or Berserker, `Wounds > 0`, and `AttachedToId` already set before `EventAttachmentEquipped` or `ownerIsAttached` is false. Technique `IsActive` waits for that host's `EventDuelEndOfRound` (+1/+1 threat). Another actor does not clear it. Cancel and duel end clear it with no threat. Resolve does not set Used. Do not set `DUEL_ID`/`ROUND` or the column note hits SQL.
- **Jägerarmbrust.** +1 gamble card only for the bearer. Engage is skipped for `IsTemporaryCopy` or `IsEffectsOnlyCopy`, but **an in-duel resolve still queues the adversary discard on a copy.** Drain that transition before asserting that a preview queues nothing. +1 threat applies on refuse and on preview. Preview queues neither discard nor `EventRangedAbilityPlayed`. Refuse adds threat and the ranged event, no discard. Calculate is +1 thrust.
- **Seek Each Devil.** Discount is action-from-hand only, Academic or Hunter, +1, and only when the action id matches. Targets are controlled enemies at adjacent city locations. Adjacency uses the `PLAYER_COUNT` global (2 players: Docks touches Forum, not Bazaar). Move `engage` false. `PlayersToDiscard` is other controllers of a Sorcerer or Monster at the destination who have a hand. Discard is `asEffect`.
- **No More Words.** Attachment must be (Weapon AND Melee) or Eisenfaust. `_04017` is Weapon+Ranged until `addTrait(Melee)` is quiet. Type 28 so the performer is not auto-engaged. Act engages the attachment only and opens `04019_2`. Does not resolve.
- **Vantage Point.** Risk must be in hand. Performer en garde, adjacent to the pressure (`includeHome` false), with Weapon+Ranged equipped, and an opponent at the pressure location. Use queues `EventEnteringPayState` with `payStateType` `PAY_STATE_IN_HAND_REACTION` and does not set Used. The second transition is `pay`. After `EventRiskReactionTriggered`, one opponent is auto-penalized (`VANTAGE_POINT_PRESSURE_TYPE` ORed onto `PRESSURE_TYPE`, `VANTAGE_POINT_PLAYER_ID`). `performerId` is public. Engaged or a risk that left the hand is not offered.

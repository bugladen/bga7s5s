## Text clauses
1. **Reaction:** After a Hunter or Berserker equips this card • They heal a wound. → Pattern D AttachmentReaction on `EventAttachmentEquipped`.
2. **Gambling Technique:** At the end of your round, each participant gains a threat. → Pattern E Gambling Technique; activate on Resolve, apply on `EventDuelEndOfRound`.

## Pattern choices / WHY
- Not Pattern A equip restriction — text does NOT say "may only equip to Hunter/Berserker"; anyone can equip, Reaction only offers when host has Hunter OR Berserker + Wounds > 0.
- Heal is optional Reaction (Pass button), not Forced — keyword is Reaction.
- Threat: `createThreatModifiedEvent(1, 1)` mirrors Reaction_02039 "both participants". Deferred via IsActive + EndOfRound actorId == owning character ("your round"), not immediate on Resolve. Clear IsActive on TechniqueCanceled / DuelEnd / after fire.
- Gambling gates: `DUEL_GAMBLED` + actor identity (Pattern E) — not a trait.
- No engage cost printed on either ability.
- Invigorant missing from TraitNames — add alphabetically (after Inspiring).

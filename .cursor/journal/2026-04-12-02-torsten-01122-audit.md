## Torsten Vakt (01122) Audit

Audited card 01122 - Torsten Vakt, "Incorrigible Drunk" (Ussura faction).

Card text has two abilities:
1. While Torsten has 2+ wounds → his combat cards gain +1 Thrust (passive)
2. Reaction: When a Sorcery or Sorcerer ability targets Torsten → Cancel the effects

**Both abilities correctly implemented. No fixes needed.**

Key finding on the Sorcery/Sorcerer ability question: All Sorcery cards (Risk cards with "Sorcery" trait) have actions that implement `ISorcererAbility`. So both "Sorcery" and "Sorcerer ability" targeting fire `EventSorcererAbilityStart` with a `targetId`. The reaction's single event check covers both cases. Verified by checking Action_01025, Action_01030, Action_01085, Action_01133, Action_01161, Action_01172, and Maneuver_01133 — all implement `ISorcererAbility` and the character-targeting ones pass `$character->Id` as `targetId`.

The +1 Thrust passive correctly uses `EventDuelCalculateCombatCardStats` with `actorId == this->Id` (meaning Torsten is playing the combat card) and `addThrust(1)` which respects dashed values.

The reaction uses `stackEvent` (not `queueEvent`) for the transition — this gives it priority to interrupt before the ability resolves. On cancel, it calls `deleteEventsTargetingCard` and `deleteTransitionEventsBySourceId` to clean up.

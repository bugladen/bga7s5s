## Text → pattern
`<b>En Garde Musketeer Action:</b> Target an opposing character • Move them and your performer to a <b>City</b> location you control, or one where you control a <b>Leader</b>.`

- Plain Action → Pattern B `RiskAction`; `parent::getPerformersForAction` (home eligible).
- En Garde → `!$Engaged` precondition (not Engage cost).
- Musketeer → mechanical `hasTrait("Musketeer")` — not Sorcerer.
- Printed **Target** opposing character → Cesca (`IAbilityThatTargetsCharacters` + Risk `IRiskThatTargetsCharacters`).
- Opposing = same location + other controller.
- Then location chooser: any City location (NOT adjacent-only — text omits adjacent) where claim-control is you OR your Leader sits there.
- Move both with `engage=false` (no Engage in bullet). Mirror Tea and Cakes `_02025` / Giacinto `_01205` move-both + batchId.

## WHY not B.1 / B.5 alone
B.1 is location-only (no character Target). B.5 moves only the performer to the target's adjacent location. Depose is character Target then free City destination filtered by control/Leader — closest is `_02025` two-step with different destination predicate.

## Destination quirks (WHYs)
- Exclude current location: "Move … to" is a no-op if same spot; also avoids offering a dead chooser when already at the only controlled loc without a Leader elsewhere.
- Do not require adjacency (unlike `_02025` / Giacinto).
- Claim-control via `getControllerForLocation == playerId`; Leader via `getLeaderByPlayerId` Location match (you "control a Leader" there).
- Grey Action when no legal destination exists (performer filter), same discipline as B.1.

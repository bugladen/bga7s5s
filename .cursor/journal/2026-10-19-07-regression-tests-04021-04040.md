## WHYs pinned

- **Technique_04021a.** Duel + she is the actor + another controlled Musketeer at her location has a printed technique. Her own `Technique_04021a`, temporary copies, attachment techniques, and a trait-only Musketeer do not count. Resolve only opens `"04021"`. The copy is `IsTemporaryCopy`, `CHOSEN_TECHNIQUE_IS_MAIN` false, and immediately activated/resolved/calculated.
- **Technique_04021b.** Engaged hides it. In a duel the actor must be her. Parent +1 Thrust is challenge threat and duel thrust.
- **Reaction_04021.** Opponent source (controller neither hers nor 0) engaging her other Musketeer here. `sourceId` 0 and her own card do not. En Garde does not `setUsed`. Controller 0 returns first.
- **Axelle.** Flag ±1, stats are not rewritten. A Duelist moving in is read off the event because the location list is stale. Leaving clears. Controller 0 returns before any stamp. Approach uses `event->playerId` while `ControllerId` is still 0. Blanking clears only when controller is non-zero — controller 0 leaves a stuck flag. Locker skips the update. Home is shared, so an opponent arriving at `LOCATION_PLAYER_HOME` does not count (covered by the approach/home unblank path using her own Duelist).
- **Reaction_04022.** Opposing combat card, in a duel, she is in the city at a participant's location. En garde threats both; engaged threats only hers. Pass does not spend. Home and her own announcement do not offer.
- **Reaction_04023.** She must move to a city location, en garde, with a deck card. Pass and reveal do not `setUsed` (requeue would die). Sink of a non-attachment finishes and sinks `onTop` false. A WealthCost 0 revealed attachment equips (`EventAttachmentEquipping`) and finishes when nothing remains.
- **Diplomatic Envoy.** Forum renown always. Diplomat pick only if one is not already at the Forum. Move `engage` false. `eventCheck` is called directly: Combat vs an en garde Forum Diplomat while the scheme is at Home throws. Finesse, engaged, and a scheme that left Home do not.
- **04025 scheme.** City card when the city deck has one; the Renown pick still opens when it does not. Bazaar is not a legal Renown location. `nextState()` is bare.
- **Reaction_04025.** Home + at least one Merchant + a deck card. Look count is 3 + merchants. ≤2 draws them all. 3+ opens `"04025"` and requires exactly two ids; the rest sink to the bottom. Pass does not `setUsed`.
- **Pompon.** Equip check is direct. Diplomat only. Technique engages the attachment unless `IsTemporaryCopy` or `IsEffectsOnlyCopy`, then +1 Parry. Bearer must be the actor; an engaged attachment hides it.
- **Reaction_04026.** Other player's success with difference ≤1, attached, same location, attachment en garde. Offer is `HIGH_PRIORITY`. Fail engages, deletes the queued pressure result, and queues a failed one. Own pressure, difference 2, and an engaged attachment do not offer. Pass does not spend.
- **04027.** En garde Diplomat. Leader is not a target. The click opens `04027_2` and does not resolve. Id 1 engages. Id 2 claims only while `canLocationBeClaimedBy`; an unclaimable location still resolves the action.
- **04028.** Destinations use `setControllerForLocation` (the `Control_` global), or the leader's city location, never the current spot. Both moves `engage` false and share a batch. The target click does not move.
- **04029.** En garde Merchant in the city. Steal unequips through play-discard, then hand, then `PAY_STATE_EQUIP_ATTACHMENT`. `CHOSEN_OPPONENT` is the old controller. A non-Villain leader (or no leader) draws; a Villain leader does not. Home hides it. Short wealth throws.
- **Action_04030.** Adjacent and strictly more Renown. Two players: Docks → Forum, not Bazaar even if the Bazaar is richer. Scoundrel/Merchant discount is +1 on this action id only. Move `engage` false. Home performers have no destination — the availability check is any performer, so a second character left at the Docks still keeps the action available.
- **Maneuver_04030.** Discount only when the combat card is this risk and the actor is Merchant or Scoundrel. Id 1 is Parry; id 2 is Thrust. Cancel resets to Thrust. Activation is `stackEvent`.
- **Technique_04031.** Actor must be Andare. +1 Riposte always. Engaged queues `EventThreatModified` with `defenderThreatIsLethal` true when he is the challenger (actor). En garde does not.
- **Reaction_04031.** Round 1, en garde, duel at his location, participant threat > 0. Remove is -1 on his side and `setUsed`. Pass does not. Round 2, zero threat, and engaged do not offer.
- **Giacinto.** Opposing Sorcerer moving in gets the condition and -1 Influence. A second pass does not stack. His leaving that location clears it. Blank and destroy clear every stamp. Controller 0 and Home do nothing. Home is an early return, not an empty opposing list that still runs.
- **Action_04032.** Target click opens `04032_2` for the opponent. Reveal stores `REVEALED_CARDS` and opens `04032_4` (second transition, the first is still queued). Discard is `asEffect` true and resolves with no move. Pass opens `04032_3`. Both moves `engage` false. Bazaar is not adjacent to the Docks at 2 players.
- **Iago.** Duel start or swap-in adds Pirate and Scoundrel once (`DuelTraitsApplied`). Swap-out and duel end remove them. A spectator duel and controller 0 do not grant them.
- **Technique_04033.** Outside a duel there is no actor gate. Resolve spends it, defaults `UseThrust` false, sets `PendingThreatChoice`, and opens `"04033"`. Id 1 is Thrust (also challenge adversary threat). Parry does nothing on a challenge. His own new round keeps the pending flag. The adversary's round clears it and prompts once. Id 1 there adds challenger threat when he is the challenger. Cancel and duel end clear the pending flag.
- **04034 scheme.** Unique fewest only; a 0–0 tie is not fewest. Pass throws until a location has Renown. Place is a normal add. Move is adjacent (`isMove` true on the second add). Bazaar is not adjacent to the Docks.
- **Action_04034.** Performer in a city an opponent controls (`CityLocation->Controller`, not the Control_ global). Trigger's active player is that controller. Id 1 uncontrols. Id 2 wounds opposing characters, not yours. The scheme controller cannot choose. Controlling it yourself hides the action.
- **04035 scheme.** Bazaar and Forum renown always. The pick needs an Academic and a non-Revelry risk in discard or locker. Pass throws while one is eligible. Revelry throws. Discard uses remove-from-discard; locker uses remove-from-locker. `nextState("")`.
- **Reaction_04035.** Your pressure, Academic at that location. Use ORs `MEETING_OF_THE_MINDS_PRESSURE_TYPE` and stores the player. Pass does not spend. Another player's pressure and a location with no Academic do not offer.
- **Ciphered Tome.** Academic only, equip check direct. **04036a** engages the tome and moves Renown to another city location (not Home, not here). Zero Renown or an engaged tome hides it. **04036b** is `getActions()[1]`. Destination is strictly more Renown, adjacency not required. Bearer move is `engage` false.
- **04037.** En garde Academic plus an uncontrolled city card that `canBeDiscardedFromCity`. Discard is `asEffect` false. Pass adds none, sinks the peeked cards `onTop` false, and sets `EXTRA_ACTIONS` / `EXTRA_ACTION_PERFORMER`. Choosing one adds it and does not sink it. An empty city deck skips the picker and still grants the extra action. Engaging the Academic hides the action — another non-Academic at the location does not keep it available, but a second Academic would.
- **Panacea card.** In-hand action pay grants Wealth to other Alquimia and Discovery cards via `getCardById` (the world copy). Itself, non-matching traits, and cards that already have Wealth are skipped. Re-entering the same pay does not double. `PAY_STATE_IN_HAND_REACTION` does not revoke. A different pay type, a payment discard (`AsPayment`, locker event, id dropped from the list), and `EventPlayerTurnEnd` do.
- **Action_04038.** Academic, not an en garde action, so an engaged performer still qualifies. Engaged or wounded targets. Both effects queue when both are true. A healthy en garde character throws.
- **Maneuver_04038.** Academic actor. Resolve en gardes only if engaged and heals one wound only if wounded. Already fine is still available and queues neither.
- **04039.** Zealot/Academic/Bard discount +1. `announceAction` engages before `EventActionActivated` and does not engage again if already engaged. Confirm loses a `CityLocation` you control and claims the performer's location. A location you do not control throws. No controlled location hides the action.
- **04040.** Academic, opposing character with a non-fake attachment. The fake stays equipped but is omitted from the attachment list and throws if clicked. The attachment click queues only `EventCharacterTargeted`. `canceled` emits nothing. A live hook unequips, lockers, wounds, and resolves on the targeting event's batch.
- **Maneuver_04040.** Academic actor and an adversary who is not in the discard/locker map. Resolve wounds that adversary.

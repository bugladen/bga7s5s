## Text clauses
1. *En Garde* — When an opposing character recruits a Mercenary, they gain +1 cost.
2. City Action: Move target Mercenary at this location Home. (in play, not available)

## Pattern choices
- **Passive:** `getParleyDiscount` -= 1 (negative discount = +1 cost). WHY: recruit cost goes through `getParleyDiscount` only (`FrameworkActionsTrait` / `stRecruitComputeDiscount`). No separate recruit-discount hook. Mirror Makepeace `_01092` equip tax shape.
- **En Garde gate:** `!$this->Engaged` — italic En Garde is a precondition, same as Astrid `_04cd04`. Not an Engage cost.
- **Opposing:** performer `isNotControlledByPlayer` + same location + `cardInCity($performer)`. WHY cardInCity: Home shares one location string; recruit performers are city-only but the city check matches Makepeace and blocks Home false-positives.
- **City Action:** Pattern C CharacterAction + `IAbilityThatTargetsCharacters` (text says "target"). Eligible = Mercenary trait + `isControlled()` at Hans's location. WHY `isControlled`: "in play and not available" = recruited, not an uncontrolled city merc sitting as recruit fodder. No "opposing" printed → own Mercenaries are legal targets.
- **Move Home:** `createCardMovingEvent(..., LOCATION_PLAYER_HOME, engage=false)` — Engage not printed. Mirror Action_01092.
- **State ID:** `404011` / transition `"04011"`. One-step picker like Makepeace.

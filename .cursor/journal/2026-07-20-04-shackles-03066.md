## WHY performer resolution (important)
BGA Equip sets `CHOSEN_PERFORMER` to the equip *target* (Legion's Caress `_01021` shape). Printed text needs two characters: your performer + opposing target.

Did **not** build a two-step UI. Instead:
- Target must be opposing (`ControllerId != equipping player`)
- You must control a character **at the target's location** with `ModifiedFinesse > target.ModifiedFinesse`

WHY same-location: wiring.md defines "opposing" as different controller AND same location. The higher-Finesse ally is the implicit performer.

Alternatives considered:
- Two-step choose-performer-then-target: more faithful to physical Equip, much more framework work
- Any ally anywhere with higher Finesse: ignores opposing same-location
- Leader as performer proxy: wrong

If Eddie says physical rules require an explicit chosen performer who also pays/engages, revisit with a dedicated picker state.

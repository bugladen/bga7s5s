# Cesca Del Rosso (01008) Audit

## Card Text
- **Sorcerer City Action:** Reveal the top card of your deck. If it is a Sorcery, put it into your hand. If not, you may sink it.
- **Reaction:** After Cesca performed a Sorcerer Ability, or one targeted a character at this location • Wound her. Cesca performs a copy of it, paying all costs. (Can choose new targets.)

## Bugs Found and Fixed

### Bug 1: Missing SorcererAbility events on Sorcery path
In `stateFromAction` at state `_01008_3`, when the revealed card IS a Sorcery (drawn to hand), no `SorcererAbilityStart`, `SorcererAbilityPlayed`, or `ActionResolved` events were fired. This meant Cesca's own reaction (and any other sorcerer-ability reactions) would never trigger when her action drew a Sorcery to hand.

**Fix:** Added all three events (`SorcererAbilityStart`, `ActionResolved`, `SorcererAbilityPlayed`) around the `CardDrawn` event in the Sorcery branch.

### Bug 2: Missing events on Pass path
When the player reveals a non-Sorcery card and chooses NOT to sink it, the state used generic `actPass` which just transitions without firing any events. No `SorcererAbilityStart`, `SorcererAbilityPlayed`, or `ActionResolved` events fired.

**Fix (three parts):**
1. Changed state `_01008_4` possibleactions from `actPass` to `actFromCardPass` so the action's pass handler gets called
2. Added `actFromActionPass()` method to `Action_01008` that fires all required events
3. Updated JS `OnUpdateActionButtons.7s5s.js` to call `actFromCardPass` instead of `actPass`

### WHY these events matter
`SorcererAbilityPlayed` is what triggers Cesca's own reaction (Reaction_01008) and any other card's reaction to sorcerer abilities. Without it, her reaction would only trigger on the Sink path - missing 2 of 3 possible outcomes. `ActionResolved` is required by the pre-commit hook for all CharacterAction subclasses. `SorcererAbilityStart` is required for ISorcererAbility implementations.

## No Issues Found
- **Reaction_01008:** Correctly triggers on `EventSorcererAbilityPlayed`, properly checks all three conditions (source is Cesca, Cesca is performer, or ability targeted character at her location), wounds as cost, copies ability, calls `setUsed()`/`isAvailable()`, cleans up on turn end.
- **Zombie handler:** Still works since it directly calls `nextState()`, unaffected by the possibleactions change.

## Text → patterns
**Leader City Action:** Wound your performer • If you control fewer locations than an opponent, pressure your performer's location with your choice of [Combat], [Finesse], or [Influence]. If successful, claim this location. Send this card to The Locker.

Classification:
- Pattern A (`RiskCityAction`) + Leader performer (no `RequiresPerformerSelected` — mirror `Action_03020`)
- Bullet-If "fewer locations than an opponent" = availability gate (strict `<` vs any opponent)
- Choose pressure stat = card-specific GameState with Combat/Finesse/Influence buttons
- Pressure + claim on success = `Action_01141` / `Action_01206` shape (`EventLocationPressureResult`)
- Locker always after pressure resolves (Unique spend — not success-gated)

## WHY choices
1. **Leader not RequiresPerformerSelected:** skill + Commanding. Set `CHOSEN_PERFORMER` ourselves for pressure pipeline (`stHighDramaPressureLocation` reads it).
2. **Wound at announce (EventActionTriggered) before choose-stat:** printed cost before the chooser, same discipline as engage-before-chooser. Wound then transition "03067".
3. **Locker always (success or fail):** Unique resource spent by playing the Action; pressure may fail but card still leaves the game. Queue on `EventLocationPressureResult` with claim + ActionResolved.
4. **No IRiskThatTargetsCharacters:** no printed "Target"; only a stat button chooser.
5. **Do not gate availability on claimability:** claim is pressure success payoff; pressure still playable when Leshiye/etc blocks (01141/01206).
6. **Stat buttons filtered by `canPressure`:** only offer non-dashed stats Leader can use.

# Smuggling Run (_03063)

## Classification
1. Pattern A trivial resolve — Bazaar + Docks Renown (sack + anchor icons on art; scaffold Text already correct)
2. Passive equip tax — `getEquipDiscount` -= 1 when opponent equips onto a city character opposing your Scoundrel. Mirror Makepeace `_01092`, but gate on *any* owned Scoundrel at the performer's city location (scheme sits at Home — not same-location as Makepeace).
3. Scoundrel City Action — SchemeCityAction + Scoundrel trait gate (NOT ISorcererAbility). Move Renown OR available attachment from performer location → different City location.

## WHY equip tax lives on scheme `getEquipDiscount`
Theah iterates every card in `$this->cards` for discounts. Chosen scheme is at `LOCATION_PLAYER_HOME` all day, so it's in the loop. Negative discount = cost increase (Makepeace pattern). Must `cardInCity($performer)` — Home shares one location string across players and would false-positive otherwise.

## WHY City Action is two HD states (not immediate)
Need: pick what to move (Renown button id=0 vs attachment card pick), then destination location. id=0 is safe for Renown — card ids never 0. Persist `$MoveMode` on action + `updateCardObjectInDb` (03029/03062 lesson). `CHOSEN_CARD` holds attachment id between states.

## Availability
Performer must be Scoundrel in city AND location must have Renown>0 OR ≥1 available attachment (`getAvailableAttachmentsAtLocation`). Destinations always exist (other city locations).

## Traits / faction
Cunning–Crime, Neutral, Init 52, Panache 0 — scaffold matches art.

## Implemented
- `_03063.php` — Pattern A resolve (Bazaar+Docks), `getEquipDiscount` Scoundrel tax, Action wired
- `Action_03063` — Scoundrel gate; state1 Renown(id=0)/attachment pick; state2 city dest; batch Renown move; `createCardMovingEvent(engage=false)` for attachment; `createActionResolvedEvent`

## WHY id=0 for Renown
Avoids collision with attachment card ids (never 0). Same sentinel idea as CHOSEN_PERFORMER=0.

# Tip the Scales (04030) Implementation

User updated printed text mid-session: discount clause changed from Scoundrel/Diplomat to **Merchant/Scoundrel**. Implemented against the updated text.

## Shape classification

1. **Dual discount paths** — "While your performer is a Merchant or Scoundrel, this card has -1 cost." Split by pay channel (framework has no single Risk-class hook at pay time):
   - City Action: `getActionFromHandDiscount` on `Action_04030` (chosen performer).
   - Combat card / Maneuver pay: `getManeuverFromCombatCardDiscount` on `Maneuver_04030` (duel participant = performer via `getDuelRoundActor()`).
   Same printed clause, two hooks — mirror `_04007` wounds discount on Maneuver for combat pay + E.2 on Action for hand pay.

2. **Pattern A / B.1 City Action** — "Move your performer to an adjacent City location with more Renown." Location chooser only — no "Target" wording, no Cesca interfaces. Inverse renown filter of `Action_02023` (less Renown on *opposing* move): here performer moves self to adjacent where `adjacent.Renown > current.Renown`. `RiskCityAction` + filter performers to those with ≥1 valid destination. Grey when performer is already at a local renown peak among neighbors.

3. **Pattern C.3 pure-calc Maneuver** — "+1 Parry or +1 Thrust." Mirror `Maneuver_03024` at +1 with no adversary-trait gate. `EventManeuverActivated` + `stackEvent` (choice before calc). `$ChooseParry` sticky on Maneuver instance; cleared on `EventManeuverCanceled`.

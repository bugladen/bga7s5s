# Amour _01104 Audit

## Card Text
- **City Action:** Your en garde performer and target opposing character both engage and go Home.

## Audit Result: Bug found and fixed

### Implementation Walkthrough

**_01104.php** — Base card class. Risk card, Castille faction, 1 Wealth cost, Parry 2 (dashed Riposte/Thrust). Implements `IHasActions` and `IRiskThatTargetsCharacters`. Single action: `Action_01104`.

**Action_01104.php** — Extends `RiskCityAction`. Flow:
1. `isAvailableToPlayer` / `getPerformersForAction` filter to en garde characters (`!$character->Engaged`) in the city
2. `handleEvent` on `EventActionTriggered` queues a transition to state `HIGH_DRAMA_PLAYER_TURN_01104`
3. `getArgsFromAction` provides `ids` of opposing characters at performer's location via `getOpposingCharactersAtLocation`
4. `actFromActionWithId` validates (controller check, location check), then:
   - Moves target → Home (engage=true)
   - Moves performer → Home (engage=true)
   - Creates actionResolvedEvent

### Bug: Performer selection didn't check for opposing characters

`isAvailableToPlayer` and `getPerformersForAction` only checked that the performer was en garde in the city. They did NOT verify that there were opposing characters at the performer's location. This meant:
- The action could appear available even if no valid targets existed
- A player could select a performer at a location with no opponents, enter the target state, and have zero valid picks

**Fix:** Added opposing-character checks to both methods, matching the pattern from Action_01056. Each candidate performer now only qualifies if `getOpposingCharactersAtLocation` returns at least one opponent at their location.

User caught this during review — I initially marked the audit clean. Lesson: always trace the full user flow (can you select → does it lead to valid choices?) not just verify the final action logic.

### WHY no setUsed/announceAction/resetPassCount in the action

These are handled by the framework for all risk-from-hand actions. `FrameworkActionsTrait.php` ~line 940-974:
- `announceAction` called at line 943
- Pass count reset at line 974
- Card itself goes to purgatory at line 939 (so setUsed is moot — card is consumed)

This matches the pattern of other simple RiskCityAction cards like Action_01055 and Action_01105, which also omit these calls.

### Engage mechanic verification

`createCardMovingEvent` 5th param is `$engage` (default true). EventHub line 635-636: `if ($event->engage && !$card->Engaged) $card->Engaged = true`. Both characters get engaged on the move. Already-engaged targets stay engaged — correct, since card text doesn't restrict target's engagement status.

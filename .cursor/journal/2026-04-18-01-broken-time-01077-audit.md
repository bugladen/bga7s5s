# Broken-Time (01077) Audit

## Card Text
"**Duelist Maneuver:** Reveal cards from your deck equal to your participant's [Finesse]. Play one as an additional combat card and sink the rest. You may use a Maneuver on it, paying all costs."

## Architecture
This is a Risk card (Montaigne, Flourish/Valroux traits) with a single Duelist Maneuver. The implementation uses a two-state flow:

### Flow
1. Maneuver activated on Broken-Time during duel → `handleEvent` queues transition to state `01077`
2. `DUEL_RESOLVE_MANEUVER_01077` (multipleactiveplayer) — Shows revealed cards to all players via public args. Active player auto-deactivated; opponents click OK.
3. `DUEL_RESOLVE_MANEUVER_01077_2` (activeplayer) — Active player picks one card from revealed set (private args).
4. `actFromManeuverWithId`:
   - Sinks unchosen cards via `insertCardOnExtremePosition(..., false)`
   - Queues `CardAddedToHandEvent` for chosen card
   - Sets `ABNORMAL_FLOW = true` (disables back buttons)
   - Sets `NEXT_COMBAT_CARD = $id`
5. Events process in `DUEL_RESOLVE_MANEUVER_EVENTS`
6. `DUEL_SET_NEXT_COMBAT_CARD` picks up the card:
   - If chosen card has maneuvers → `DUEL_USE_MANEUVER_FROM_COMBAT_CARD` (with decline option)
   - Else → `DUEL_APPLY_COMBAT_CARD_STATS` (applies Riposte/Parry/Thrust)
7. Maneuver costs handled via `DUEL_GET_MANEUVER_FROM_COMBAT_CARD_COST` → `DUEL_PAY_FOR_MANEUVER_FROM_COMBAT_CARD`

## Audit Result
No bugs found. All six aspects of the card text are correctly implemented. The ABNORMAL_FLOW mechanism prevents back-navigation during forced play. The two-phase reveal (public multiactive → private choice) is clean.

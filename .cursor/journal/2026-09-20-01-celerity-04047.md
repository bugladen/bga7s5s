# Celerity (_04047) Implementation

## Card Text
**Duelist City Action:** Engage your performer • They issue a [Finesse] challenge to target opposing character. Only characters with 3[Finesse] or more can intervene. These effects cannot be cancelled.

## Classification
- Pattern A (RiskCityAction) + engage cost (Cornered/Censure announceAction shape)
- Duelist performer gate (heading trait — not Sorcerer)
- Finesse challenge → `STAT_FINESSE` + `!DashedFinesse` (Raven)
- Cesca yes ("target opposing character")
- Custom `CELERITY_CHALLENGE_TYPE` — intervene gate only (Finesse ≥ 3), **not** refuse (differs from AJA which also blocks refuse)

## WHY not reuse AJA_CHALLENGE_TYPE
Aja printed "intervene **or refuse**"; Celerity only "can intervene". Reusing AJA would wrongly disable Refuse when defender Finesse < 3.

## WHY engage in announceAction, type off auto-engage
Same as Censure/Cornered: Night of Drinking deletes ActionTriggered; cost must survive cancel. Auto-engage list would double-engage on a non-cancelled play.

## "These effects cannot be cancelled"
User confirmed 01109 must not cancel Celerity. Added `Risk::effectsCannotBeCancelled()` (default false); `_04047` and `_02061` (same printed phrase) return true. `Reaction_01109` gates all three offer paths (ActionActivated / RiskPlayed / ManeuverActivated). WHY a Risk method not instanceof list: next uncancellable Risk opts in without growing 01109 special cases. Reimplemented after accidental revert.

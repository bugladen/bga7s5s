1. Scoundrel Maneuver: +1 Riposte. If the adversary gambles during their next round, you choose their combat card.
2. Duelist Maneuver: The adversary cannot gamble during their next round.

## Approach / WHY

### Split a/b Maneuvers
Distinct trait gates (Scoundrel vs Duelist) + very different cross-round effects. Mirror `_01108a`/`_01108b`.

### Duelist — mirror Technique_02037
`eventCheck` on `EventDuelAttemptGamble` throws when flag set for blocked adversary character. Arm on `EventResolveManeuver`. Clear on cancel / duel end / owning controller becoming actor again (`EventDuelNewRound` when actor ControllerId == owner ControllerId). WHY ControllerId not character id: Maneuver lives on a Risk in the dueling line, not an equipped Technique on a character.

### Scoundrel — +1 Riposte (calc) + cross-round choose-gamble
Arm `ChooseAdversaryGambleCombatCard` + blocked character id on resolve.

Hijack on `EventDuelGambleCardsRevealed` (not AttemptGamble — they may still attempt; we only care when cards are revealed for choosing). Queue `"03047"` transition to Maneuver owner. Transition priority 8 runs AFTER reaction transitions (6), so Ivy-style "before choosing" reactions still fire first.

### actGambleCardChosen must use actor deck, not active player
Normal flow active player == gambling actor. Our hijack makes active = Maneuver owner. Fix: always read/write the duel-round actor's faction deck; restore `changeActivePlayer(actor->ControllerId)` before `useManeuver`/`noManeuver` so subsequent combat-card maneuver UI belongs to the gambler.

### Custom state (not reuse DUEL_CHOOSE_GAMBLE_CARD)
Better descriptionMyTurn, no Back (gamble already committed), mirrors chooseList UI. `actFromCardWithId` → routing → `actGambleCardChosen`. Transitions named `useManeuver` / `noManeuver` same as stock choose state.

## Fix — announce revealed gamble names centrally
WHY: Gamble is a reveal. Names belong in the public game log even though stock `argsDuelChooseGambleCard` keeps cards in `_private` (active-only choose UI). Opponents/spectators/history otherwise never learn unchosen revealed options.

Implementation:
- `EventHub` `EventDuelGambleCardsRevealed`: `notify->all` `'${player_name} reveals the following cards for their Gamble: ${names}.'` using inject codes from `$event->revealedCardIds`
- Hub runs before cards (default `runEventHubAfterCards = false`) so the reveal line lands first
- `Maneuver_03047a` waiting log no longer re-lists `${names}` — only explains who steals the choose (avoids duplicate name spam)

Deliberate FOW change: unchosen gamble options are now public via log. Stock choose UI remains `_private`.

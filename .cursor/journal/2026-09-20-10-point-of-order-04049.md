# Point of Order (_04049) Implementation

## Card Text
**City Action:** Target an opposing character with lower [Influence] than your performer • They may engage. If they do not, they must move to an adjacent City location.

## Classification
- Pattern A `RiskCityAction` + B.7-shaped "they may engage / if not …" outcome
- Decline consequence = mandatory adjacent City move (not claim like `_04027`)
- Cesca yes — printed "Target" + character chooser
- Influence gate: strict `<` via `ModifiedInfluence` (not Arrogant `_03008` `<=`)

## Design decisions (WHY)

### Three GameStates
1. Owner picks target (`04049`)
2. Target controller Engage / Decline and Move (`04049_2`) — labeled Decline like Yield/B.7 so stake is visible
3. Target controller picks adjacent City destination (`04049_3`)

WHY not collapse 2+3 into location-as-decline: Engage must stay an explicit button; location pick is only the "if they do not" branch.

### Already Engaged → skip to `_3`
Print does **not** say "en garde" as a target filter (unlike B.7). Mirror Duckfoot `_01049`: already Engaged cannot take the engage half → auto-resolve the alternate (here: move chooser). En garde targets always get the choice even if adjacency is weirdly empty (engage still legal).

### Player swaps via EVENTS for `_2`/`_3` from step 1
Same as `_04027`: `createTransitionEvent(target->ControllerId, …)` then `characterChosen` → EVENTS. Decline `_2`→`_3` can be **direct** (same active player) — like Depose `characterChosen`→`_2`.

### ActionResolved after opponent finishes
Effect *is* engage-or-move. Do not resolve after target pick (B.7 discipline).

### Stub hygiene
`Beauracracy` → `Bureaucracy`. Add `Authority` to TraitNames (Demoralize already present).

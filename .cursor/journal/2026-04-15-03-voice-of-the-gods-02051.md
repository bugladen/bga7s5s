# Voice of the Gods (_02051) - Sorcerer City Action Implementation

## Card Ability
**Sorcerer City Action:** Engage your performer - En garde target character at your performer's location. Send this card to The Locker.

## Implementation

### Files Changed/Created
- `_02051.php` — Added `IHasActions`, `ActionTrait`, wired up `Action_02051`
- `Action_02051.php` (new) — The action class
- `State_highDramaPhase02051.php` (new) — State for target character selection
- `States.php` — Added `HIGH_DRAMA_PLAYER_TURN_02051 = 402051`

### Design Decisions

**Performer filter:** `hasTrait("Sorcerer") && !Engaged`. WHY: Card says "Sorcerer City Action" and "Engage your performer" — performer must be an unengaged Sorcerer. Did not filter on "Galdr" specifically because the card text says "Sorcerer City Action" not "Galdr City Action". The Galdr/Storte Merke traits are on the Risk card itself, not a performer restriction.

**Target selection:** Any character at performer's location except the performer themselves. WHY: Card text says "target character at your performer's location" without "opposing" or "enemy" qualifier. Could target friendly or enemy characters. The performer is excluded because engaging and en-gardering the same character would be contradictory.

**Availability check:** Requires at least one unengaged Sorcerer at a location with at least one other character. Both `isAvailableToPlayer` and `getPerformersForAction` check this so performers without valid targets aren't offered.

**Event order:** sorceryStart -> engage performer -> en garde target -> send to locker -> sorceryPlayed -> actionResolved. WHY: Start/played bracket the sorcery effects. Locker happens after the mechanical effects (engage/en garde) but before sorcery resolution, matching how other "send to locker" cards work (e.g., Action_01168).

**No `removeFromHand` event before locker:** Unlike Action_01168 which calls `createCardRemovedFromPlayerDiscardPileEvent` before the locker event, this card is played from hand through the standard action activation flow which already handles removing it. The `createCardSentToLockerEvent` handler in EventHub moves the card to the locker location directly.

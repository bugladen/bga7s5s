# Leja Juska (01203) Audit

## Card Text
> Negotiable (You may parley when paying for this card.)
> **Reaction:** When a duel occurs at Leja's location, at the beginning of the first round • Add or remove a threat from one of the participants.

## Bug 1: `setUsed` called unconditionally — even on pass

`performReaction` had `$this->setUsed($game->theah, true)` after all the if-blocks, meaning it fired regardless of which button was clicked — including "Pass". If a player saw the reaction prompt and passed, the reaction was consumed for the rest of the day. Every comparable reaction (01196, 01199, 01127, 01032) does NOT mark as used when the player passes.

Fix: Wrapped `setUsed` in `if ($reactionId != 'pass')`. This matches the pattern used by Reaction_01199 and others.

## Bug 2: Notification/button text "to" instead of "from" when removing threat

Three places said "Threat to" when removing threat, but should say "Threat from":
- Button text for defender remove: `-1 Threat to %s` → `-1 Threat from %s` (challenger button was already correct)
- Notification for removeThreatChallenger: `remove 1 Threat to` → `remove 1 Threat from`
- Notification for removeThreatDefender: `remove 1 Threat to` → `remove 1 Threat from`

# Honorable `_04057`

## Context
Continuing BAS Neutral Risks after Greed `_04056`. Honorable is WealthCost 1 combat card with a single En Garde Leader Action (no Maneuver/Reaction).

## Classification
`<b>En Garde Leader Action:</b> If you control fewer characters than an opponent • Your performer issues a challenge to target opposing non-<b>Leader</b> controlled by that opponent, using your choice of [Combat], [Finesse], or [Influence].`

Composition (new Pattern B.11):
1. **En Garde** → `!$Engaged` precondition on performer (not Engage cost — no `createCardEngagedEvent` in announce).
2. **Leader Action** → `RequiresPerformerSelected` + `hasTrait("Leader")` filter. **WHY not `getLeaderByPlayerId` alone:** more than one Leader can be in play (Bravos muster, etc.). Mirror `Action_01072` / `Action_02014`.
3. **Fewer characters If** → exists opponent with strict `>` in-play character count (`getCharactersInPlayByPlayerId`) — same discipline as A.8 fewer-locations, not fewest-overall.
4. **Target opposing non-Leader controlled by that opponent** → Cesca (`IRiskThatTargetsCharacters` + `IAbilityThatTargetsCharacters`); opposing = same location; controller must have more characters than you; `!hasTrait("Leader")` on target.
5. **Choice of Combat/Finesse/Influence** → choose-stat GameState (Ambitious buttons) then shared challenge chooser. Filter dashed stats via `canPressure`-equivalent.

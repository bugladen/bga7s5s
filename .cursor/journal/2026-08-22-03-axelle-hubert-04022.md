## WHY decisions

### Aura: flag ±1, not Angeline absolute
Angeline sets `Influence + bonus` absolute. Axelle buffs **two** stats that attachments also mutate — absolute would wipe attachment mods on every recount. Public `$DuelistAuraApplied` + ±1 Finesse/Influence only on transition (Benci/Ise).

### Aura: "a Duelist" not "another"
Axelle is Scoundrel/Diplomat — not Duelist. Count any controlled Duelist at location (includes self only if she somehow gains the trait). Angeline stale-DB exclude-out/include-in on `EventCardMoved`. Home via `getCharactersAtHomeByPlayerId`.

### Reaction: CardReaction not RiskReaction
02039/01135 are in-hand Risks. Axelle is in-play Character — Accept/Pass + `setUsed`. No state/JS.

### Reaction: threat mapping
`createThreatModifiedEvent(challenger, defender)`. "Your participant" = duel participant whose ControllerId == Axelle's controller. En Garde = `!$owner->Engaged` (rider, not cost) → also +1 adversary. Owner need not be the participant.

### Reaction: gates
`IN_DUEL` + `isAvailable` + announcer `playerId != owner.ControllerId` + owner controller has a duel participant. Not City Reaction.

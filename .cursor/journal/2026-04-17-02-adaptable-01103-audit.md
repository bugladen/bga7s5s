# Adaptable (01103) Audit

## Context
Continuing card audits. Previous session fixed Night of Drinking discard bug (PHP 8 loose comparison chain).

## Card Text
- **Pirate City Action:** If you are the first player - Claim your performer's location.
- **City Action:** If you are not the first player - En garde your performer.
- **Maneuver:** +2 Riposte. Then choose and gain: +2 Parry or +2 Thrust.

## Audit Results

All three abilities are correctly implemented.

**Action_01103a (Pirate City Action):** Extends RiskCityAction, checks first player, filters performers to Pirate-trait characters in city, claims performer's location. Clean.

**Action_01103b (City Action):** Extends RiskCityAction, implements IAbilityThatDependsOnNotBeingFirstPlayer, checks NOT first player (with OVERRIDE_AS_NOT_FIRST_PLAYER support), filters to Engaged characters, en gardes the performer. The Engaged filter is correct — en garde sets Engaged=false (confirmed via EventHub handler at line 532), so the character must be Engaged first.

**Maneuver_01103:** Adds +2 Riposte unconditionally, transitions to player-choice state for Parry vs Thrust, applies +2 of the chosen stat. Resets on cancel and end of round. Frontend buttons correctly labeled.

## Fix Applied
Only issue: comments in Maneuver_01103.php said "+1 Parry" and "+1 Thrust" but actual values are +2. Fixed the comments. No functional bugs found.

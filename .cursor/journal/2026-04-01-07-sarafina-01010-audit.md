# Sarafina (_01010) Audit

## Card Text
> When Sarafina gambles, reveal an additional card.
> Technique: Look at the top card of the adversary's deck, or two cards instead if you control a Strega at this location. Sink any number of them.

Vodacce Character. Resolve=5, Combat=2, Finesse=2, Influence=1. Traits: Spy, Vodacce. Implements `IHasTechniques` with `Technique_01010`.

## Bug Found: Strega location check was too broad

### What was wrong
The technique checked for Strega characters using `getCharactersInPlayByPlayerId()`, which returns ALL characters controlled by the player across every city location AND home. The card text says "if you control a Strega **at this location**" — meaning only Strega at the same location as Sarafina should count.

### Fix
Changed both `getArgsFromTechnique` and `actFromTechniqueWithIds` in `Technique_01010.php`:
- `getCharactersInPlayByPlayerId($owner->ControllerId)` → `getCharactersAtLocationByPlayerId($owner->Location, $owner->ControllerId)`

This restricts the Strega check to only characters at Sarafina's location, matching the card text.

### WHY this matters
Without the fix, a player with a Strega at any location (even Home or a different city location) would get to look at 2 cards instead of 1. The card is designed to reward having a Strega co-located with Sarafina, creating a positional synergy.

## Verified Correct

### Gamble reveal: "When Sarafina gambles, reveal an additional card"
- `getNumberOfGambleCardsToReveal` checks `$actor->Id == $this->Id` — only applies when Sarafina herself is the gambler.
- Adds +1 to the base count (base is 2 from `Theah::getNumberOfGambleCardsToReveal`).

### Technique sink: "Sink any number of them"
- `insertCardOnExtremePosition($id, $deckName, false)` moves cards to the bottom of the adversary's faction deck = sink.
- JS uses selection mode 2 (multiple select) + Pass button, allowing player to choose any subset including zero.

### State/JS
- State `DUEL_CHOOSE_TECHNIQUE_01010` is `activeplayer` type.
- JS enters show cards in chooseList, multi-select, Confirm + Pass buttons.
- On leaving, cleans up chooseList properly.

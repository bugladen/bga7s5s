## Context

## Classification
`While your performer is a Merchant or Scoundrel, this card has -1 cost.`
→ **Pattern E.2** Action-only discount (`getActionFromHandDiscount`). No Maneuver printed — do not invent one for combat-card pay.

`<b>En Garde Action:</b> If your performer is a non-Hero • They recruit target available Mercenary at their location, paying all costs. They may parley without engaging.`
→ **Pattern B** En Garde Action + recruit into shared Recruit pipeline (Cirilo/Kaspar shape), not a new GameState trio.

## WHY shared recruit + new RECRUIT_TYPE
Cirilo (`Action_01009`) already jumps into `HIGH_DRAMA_RECRUIT_ACTION_CHOOSE_MERCENARY` with `CIRILO_RECRUIT_TYPE`. Kaspar uses `KASPAR_RECRUIT_TYPE` and skips engage-on-parley by never setting `PERFORMER_PARLEYED` (own parley state → pay).

Silver Tongue needs the **normal Parley Yes/No prompt** (discount) but **must not Engage** when Yes. NORMAL path engages in `actHighDramaRecruitActionMercenaryChosen` when `PERFORMER_PARLEYED`. So mint `SILVER_TONGUE_RECRUIT_TYPE` and skip that engage branch.

Transition `"04059"` → `HIGH_DRAMA_RECRUIT_ACTION_PARLEYABLE` (not straight to choose merc — Cirilo skips parley because he already Engaged as Action cost).

## WHY hide Back like Cirilo
Once the Risk is paid and ActionTriggered fires, Back from shared recruit would land on choose-performer / undo-engage paths that assume basic Recruit. Same as Cirilo: no Back on choose merc / pay. Also hide Back on parley for this type (args need `recruitType`).

## Cesca / targets
Printed **"target"** Mercenary → `IAbilityThatTargetsCharacters` + `IRiskThatTargetsCharacters`. Cirilo used `IAbilityThatTargetsCards` historically; skill says character chooser → Characters. Shared choose-merc UI does not emit `EventCharacterTargeted` (Cirilo same) — interface still required for Rules Team / Unyielding marker.

## En Garde / non-Hero
En Garde = `!$Engaged` precondition (not Engage cost). Bullet-If non-Hero = performer filter (availability), same discipline as A.6 headcount Ifs. Performer must be in City with ≥1 available Mercenary (`!isControlled && Mercenary`).

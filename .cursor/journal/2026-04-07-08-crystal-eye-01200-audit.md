# Crystal Eye (01200) Audit

## Card Text
> **Forced:** After you equip this card • Look at an opponent's Approach Deck and choose a card in it.
> **Reaction:** After an opponent plays the chosen card during any Day • Gain a Renown.

## Verdict: Functionality Correct, Three Minor Bugs Fixed

The core logic is solid. The Forced ability properly hooks `EventAttachmentEquipped`, transitions through two states (choose opponent → choose card from their approach deck), marks the card with `CRYSTAL_EYE_TARGET`, and cleans up on unequip. The Reaction fires on `EventApproachCharacterPlayed`, `EventCharacterMustered`, and `EventSchemeCardRevealed` when they match the chosen card.

## Bugs Fixed

### 1. `getReactionDescription` undefined variable (`Reaction_01200.php`)
`$card` was only assigned inside an `if ($skull instanceof _01200)` block but referenced unconditionally in the `return`. PHP 8.4 would throw a warning/error on the undefined variable access and potentially crash on `$card->Name` if null. Fixed by initializing `$cardName = '?'` before the `if` block and adding null safety on the `getCardById` result.

Also renamed `$skull` to `$attachment` — the variable name was a copy-paste artifact from another card and made no sense for Crystal Eye.

### 2. Missing space in description
`'Chosen card %s was played.${you} may choose to gain Renown: '` — missing space between period and `${you}`. Would render as "played.You" with no space.

### 3. Double "been" typo (`_01200.php`)
Unequip notification said "has been been removed as a target" — doubled word.

## Design Notes

- The reaction's `isAvailable()` check means it fires at most once per day (reset on `EventDuskEndOfDay`). This is fine since an opponent can only play one card from their approach deck per day anyway.
- `EventCharacterMustered` being included as a trigger alongside `EventApproachCharacterPlayed` is slightly broader than the literal card text ("plays the chosen card"), but makes sense — if the chosen card enters play by mustering, it should still count.
- `EventSchemeCardRevealed` covers schemes in the approach deck — "revealing" a scheme is the scheme equivalent of "playing" a character.

# Rage (_05Hulk) City Action

Printed text:

City Action: Engage your performer. Wound them unless they are a Berserker • They issue an unrefusable [Combat] challenge to target opposing character. The adversary's first combat has -2 [Parry]. These effects cannot be cancelled.

## Shape

One City Action. No Maneuver. Closest mirrors: Celerity `_04047` (engage at announce, custom type off auto-engage, shared choose-target, cannot-cancel) and Unsanctioned `_02061` / Stand Your Ground `_04045` (unrefusable = refuse throw + zombie accept + grey Refuse). First-combat-card penalty mirrors Rattle `_04009`, but -2 Parry on the adversary, not +1 Riposte on our Duelist.

## WHY engage and wound at announce

Both clauses are before the bullet, so both are costs. Engage at announce (not on Triggered, not in stSetupChallenge) so a later cancel still paid it, and the type stays off `stIssueChallenge`'s auto-engage list. Unsanctioned engages again in `stSetupChallenge`; Rage must not, or the performer is engaged twice. Wound is the same cost, skipped only when the performer `hasTrait("Berserker")`. Heading is not "Berserker City Action" — Berserker is not a performer gate.

## WHY a new challenge type

Printed Engage means we pay it ourselves. `NORMAL` would engage again. Unrefusable is not Aja (Aja also locks low-Finesse refuse) and not Celerity (intervene-only). `RAGE_CHALLENGE_TYPE = 35` only blocks refuse. Back on choose-target stays hidden because Back is NORMAL-only — correct after an irreversible engage+wound.

## WHY the -2 is armed on ChallengeIssued, not Triggered

The adversary does not exist until the target is chosen. `EventChallengeIssued.abilityId` is `TRANSITION_INTERNAL_ID`, which a technique overwrites. Gate on `CHALLENGE_TYPE` + `CHOSEN_ACTION` (same reason as Unsanctioned's threat). Persist `FirstCombatParryCharacterId` with `IsUpdated` because the Risk is in discard by the time the duel calculates cards (`buildCity` reloads it).

Intervene replaces the defender and does not fire `EventChallengeAccepted`. Update the stored id when `oldTargetId` matches. Apply `removeParry(2)` on the first `EventDuelCalculateCombatCardStats` for that actor, then clear. Clear unused arms on `EventDuelEnd` and on `EventActionResolved` when `!IN_DUEL` (cancel/fizzle). Do not clear while `IN_DUEL` — duel-end `ActionResolved` runs after `IN_DUEL` is already false, which is fine because the card was either spent or the duel is over.

"First combat" = first combat card. There is no separate "first round only" hook, and `_04009` is the printed sibling.

## WHY cannot-cancel is just the Risk override

`Reaction_01109` already skips `effectsCannotBeCancelled()`. No new hook.

Cesca copies Sorcerer abilities from an allow-list. Rage is not Sorcerer. Still mark `IRiskThatTargetsCharacters` because the print says "target" and the chooser is a character.

CAD prototype: plain strings, no `clienttranslate`. Transition key `"05Hulk"` → shared choose-target. No card GameState.

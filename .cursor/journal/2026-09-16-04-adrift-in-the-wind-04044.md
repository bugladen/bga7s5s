**Passive on scheme, not Leader** — Scheme aura while at Home; clear on `EventCardSentToLocker`. Condition `ADRIFT_IN_THE_WIND_CONDITION` for tooltip (Contempt/Forged), `hasCondition` idempotent apply/clear.

**Scheme already at Home on ResolveScheme** — `stPlanningPhaseApproachCardsPlayed` moves chosen schemes to Home before resolve, so `isSchemeInPlay()` is true during `EventResolveScheme` (same as Contempt apply-on-resolve).

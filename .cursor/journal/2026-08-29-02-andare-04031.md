# Andare de Castillo _04031

Finished BAS character with En Garde Reaction + Technique.

## Abilities

1. **En Garde Reaction** — duel at location, beginning of round 1, remove 1 threat from your participant.
2. **Technique** — +1 Riposte; Lethal if Andare is engaged (ready).

## Decisions

**Reaction trigger = `EventDuelNewRound` round 1** — same window as Leja `Reaction_01203`, not `EventDuelStarted`. Text says "beginning of the first round."

**En Garde = `!$owner->Engaged`** at trigger and re-check in `performReaction`. Not an Engage cost.

**Valid-target gate** — only queue transition if owner controls a participant with threat > 0. Avoids useless Pass-only prompt (Pattern D).

**"Your participant"** — map to challenger or defender by `ControllerId`; remove via `createThreatModifiedEvent(-1, 0)` or `(0, -1)`. No choice between participants (unlike Leja add/remove both).

**Technique extends `Technique_PlusOneRiposte`** — reuse +1 Riposte calc; override `isAvailableToPlayer` for actor-is-owner (parent only gates IN_DUEL).

**Lethal rider checks `$owner->Engaged`** — printed "If Andare is engaged" means ready (sideways), opposite of En Garde. Queue `createGainLethalEvent` on `EventDuelCalculateTechniqueValues` when true. No `EventGenerateChallengeThreat` — duel-only technique like Benci `Technique_04001` shape.

**Fixed Text HTML** — stub had `<p<b>Technique:` typo.

No states/JS — button Reaction + inline Technique only.

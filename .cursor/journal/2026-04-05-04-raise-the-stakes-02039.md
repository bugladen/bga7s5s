# Raise the Stakes (_02039) — Reaction + Maneuver Implementation

## Card Text
- **Reaction:** When the adversary announces their combat card -- Add a threat to both participants.
- **Maneuver:** At the end of the round -- Add a threat to both participants.

## Implementation

First TAC Risk card with both `IHasReactions` and `IHasManeuvers`. Pattern follows `_01135` (Mireli's Revision) which is also a Risk card implementing both interfaces.

### No Custom States Needed
Both effects are fully automatic — no player choices beyond the initial reaction activate/pass and maneuver selection. This is much simpler than _02038 which needed a custom `State_duelResolveManeuver_02038` for the adversary's choice. Here, the effects just queue `createThreatModifiedEvent(1, 1)` which the EventHub handles entirely.

### Reaction — Hand Location Check (Risk Reaction)
The reaction fires on `EventCombatCardAnnounced`. Key gate: `$owner->Location == Game::LOCATION_HAND`. This is a risk reaction — it fires from the hand, same pattern as `Reaction_01135` (Mireli's Revision). Also gates on `$event->playerId != $owner->ControllerId` to ensure it's the adversary's card being announced.

WHY `LOCATION_HAND`: Eddie corrected an initial assumption that the card would fire from the dueling line. Risk reactions fire from hand — the card doesn't need to be the active combat card. This matches the `Reaction_01135` pattern exactly.

### Maneuver — Deferred End-of-Round Effect
Uses the `$IsActive` flag pattern from `Maneuver_01031`. On `EventResolveManeuver`, sets `$IsActive = true`. On `EventDuelEndOfRound`, if active, queues `createThreatModifiedEvent(1, 1)` and resets the flag.

No trait gate — card text says "Maneuver:" not "Pirate Maneuver:" or "Duelist Maneuver:", so it's available to any participant.

### Threat — Both Participants
`createThreatModifiedEvent(1, 1)` adds +1 to challenger threat and +1 to defender threat. This covers "both participants" regardless of which player owns the card.

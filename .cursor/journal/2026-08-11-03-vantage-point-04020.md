## Text → patterns
Single **En Garde Reaction** clause with three mechanical pieces:

1. **Trigger/gates:** `EventPressureOccuring` at a location adjacent to an en garde performer (`!$Engaged`) equipped with Weapon+Ranged attachment. Mirror `_02004` adjacent scan + `_01055`/`Maneuver_01055` ranged-weapon check.
2. **Target opponent -1 total:** Pattern D.2.1 inverted — mint `VANTAGE_POINT_PRESSURE_TYPE` (32768) + `VANTAGE_POINT_PLAYER_ID`, apply `-1` in `pressureLocation()` after Loyal block. Defer flag to post-pay `EventRiskReactionTriggered` (cancel-safe).
3. **Then wound target character unless engage:** Pattern D.1 multi-stage via reaction buttons (no GameStates). Owner picks opponent (if >1), then target character at pressure location (`IAbilityThatTargetsCharacters` / `IRiskThatTargetsCharacters`). Target controller chooses Engage vs wound — mirror `Action_01049` / `Action_01156`. Already-Engaged → auto-wound.

**No Cesca for "Target opponent"** — player chooser only (`_04009` discipline).

## Zombie WHY notes
- **01011 / 01015**: `actBack()` — same as old ZombieTrait cluster with Armed and Marshaled etc.
- **01012 / 01017 / 01019 / 01020**: `nextState("zombie")` — no Back; named zombie escape.
- **01013**: bare `nextState()` — sole `""` transition.
- **01016**: bare `nextState()` — sole `""` (mirrors 01098).
- **01016_2**: OLD ZombieTrait grouped with 01016 and called `actPass()` — broken under empty-transition rule (transitions are `pass` / `cardChosen` only). New zombie picks first thug from private args or `actFromCardPass()`. WHY not just `actPass("pass")`: card's `actFromCardPass` rejects pass while Red Hand Thugs remain in deck — would throw if zombie skipped that gate.
- **01016_3**: multiplayer acknowledge → `setPlayerNonMultiactive(..., 'multipleOk')`; `onEnteringState` calls `stMultiPlayerInitCardRevealAcknowledgeSansInitiatingPlayer` (same pattern as 01006_2 / 01008).

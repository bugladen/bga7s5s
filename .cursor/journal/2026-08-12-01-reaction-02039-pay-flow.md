# Reaction_02039 — Missing Pay / Discard Flow

## Bug
Raise the Stakes reaction was applying threat immediately on "Add Threat" without entering `playerPayForReaction` or discarding the Risk from hand.

## Root Cause
`Reaction_02039` extended `CardReaction` instead of `RiskReaction`. Hand-based Risk reactions must queue `createEnteringPayStateEvent(PAY_STATE_IN_HAND_REACTION)` + `createReactionPayTransitionEvent` from `performReaction`, then do the effect in `EventRiskReactionTriggered` after `FrameworkActionsTrait` pays and discards the card as played.

The original implementation journal (2026-04-05) said to follow `Reaction_01135` pattern but the class was scaffolded as `CardReaction`. That works for character/attachment reactions (01098) but not faction-hand Risk cards.

## Fix
- Extend `RiskReaction`
- Move threat + notify + `setUsed` into `EventRiskReactionTriggered` handler
- On `addThreat` in `performReaction`, queue pay transition events only

Same pattern as `Reaction_02060b`, `Reaction_03035`.

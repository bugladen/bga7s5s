# _01061 Risk Audit

Eddie asked to audit `_01061` against the abilities in its `Text` property and fix any issues in the component classes. This is Well-Equipped, a Risk with one Action and one Maneuver.

Important orientation: I could not read the advertised create-risk skill from the path in the startup context even though glob could see a similarly named entry. I proceeded by inspecting the card/component classes and nearby Risk/maneuver patterns directly. Previous journal notes today were relevant: the thrust clamp work reminded me to treat maneuver stat fields carefully and not assume the displayed combat stat name was harmless.

## Findings and WHY

The Action was already basically correct. The text says "En garde your equipped performer." `Action_01061` is a `RiskAction`, so the from-hand restriction comes from the base class. It requires performer selection, filters to Eddie's in-play engaged characters with at least one attachment, validates the same conditions again on trigger, then queues `createCardEngardedEvent` and `createActionResolvedEvent`. I left it alone because adding a state or target interface would be needless ceremony for a direct performer action already supported by the shared action flow.

The Maneuver was wrong in the exact place that matters: the card text says "+1 Riposte" but the component name said "+1 Thrust, Draw Card" and the implementation added to `$event->parry`. This was not a UI-only typo; it would apply the wrong duel stat. Fixed it to add `$event->riposte += 1`, renamed the maneuver, and added an explanation entry like nearby stat maneuvers. I reused the `$owner` lookup for the draw event so the method stays simple.

I left the draw-card condition intact. It uses `EventDuelCalculateManeuverValues::$actorId`, which is the duel round actor/participant for the maneuver, then checks that participant's attachments for the `Weapon` trait. That matches "your participant is equipped with a Weapon." I had a moment of doubt about `owner->ControllerId` versus `event->playerId`, but this card's owner/controller is the player resolving the maneuver, and nearby draw effects commonly use the owner/controller for card-owned reasons.

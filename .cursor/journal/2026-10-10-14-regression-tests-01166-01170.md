## WHY decisions

### Maneuver_01166 "other" cards
Journal 2026-03-31-08 already fixed counting itself. Pin: owner on line alone → +0; two siblings → +2; opponent line ignored. The unset($cards[$owner->Id]) is the whole point — don't "simplify" to count().

### Action_01167 vs Robbery (01113)
Similar discard-pile equip flow but Liberating Goods keeps attachment in discard until step 4 (remove + equip), whereas Robbery pulls to hand then pays. Also filters Unique. Pin wealth pay AsPayment=true / asEffect=false on step 4.

### Action_01168 vs Action_01139
01168 queues remove-from-discard + CardSentToLocker + ActionResolved on trigger. 01139 uses goToLocker stamp + deferred path (ActionResolved was the bug; fixed 2026-10-10-09). Pin ActionResolved count 1 here as the "correct Unique spend→Locker" reference.

### _01169 EscapeDuel
Card-only Forced (no composite). Arms on combat-card stats for this Id; EOR wounds + moves Home engaged; characterIsInDiscardOrLocker skips both; cancelEscape (Reaction_01109 edge) disarms. abilityId = (string) card Id — Joern/Stranahan comment in prod.

### _01170 Wealth locker
AsPayment → locker. AsPlayed / plain discard → no locker (journal 2026-03-31-10). Action discard hand is cost (asEffect false) so Reaction_01099a does not fire. Opulence itself is out of hand before trigger (parked), so not in the discard loop.

### Neutral faction
None of 01166–01170 call initializeFaction. Multi-faction shared Risks stay Neutral from Card::__construct. Pin Neutral — don't "fix" by adding Castille/Vodacce.

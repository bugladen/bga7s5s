Influence challenge → `STAT_INFLUENCE` + filter `!DashedInfluence` (01033/02028/Sanjay). Engage cost → `!Engaged` in availability + performers. Shared `HIGH_DRAMA_CHALLENGE_ACTION_CHOOSE_TARGET` — no new state/JS.

Did not gate availability on claimability: challenge still works when you already control the location or Leshiye blocks claim.

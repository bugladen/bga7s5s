## What

## Text (final)

1. Passive: opposed by 2+ wounded → +1 Combat
2. Technique: look top 2 own deck; may sink one or both; return others **in any order**

Eddie corrected mid-session from "same order" → "any order". That forced the 04cd15-style reorder step instead of Technique_01010 sink-only.

## WHY decisions

**Flag ±1 for Combat bonus (not absolute Combat+1):** printed Combat is 2 and attachments can also bump ModifiedCombat. Absolute recompute like Angeline's Influence (base + bonus) would wipe attachment Combat. Ise's `$WoundedCombatBonusApplied` flag is the right shape.

**Home → 0 opposing:** LOCATION_PLAYER_HOME is one string for everyone. Naïve getOpposingCharactersAtLocation(HOME) counts enemies sitting at *their* Homes. You can't be "opposed" at Home in this game — short-circuit to 0.

**Wound event order compensation:** EventCharacterWounded has runEventHubAfterCards=true, but among cards the wounded character may not have handled yet when Benci runs. Use `!characterHandled` to add/subtract event wounds for the count. Same for Heal.

**Private look args:** Technique says Look at (not Reveal). Mirrored 03052 / dusk Yevgeni private chooseList. Public notify only that a look happened + named sinks.

**Immediate deck insert for sink:** Action_04cd15 comment — queued createCardAddedToFactionDeckEvent races finishReplaceOrReorder top inserts. Bottom-insert immediately.

**createTechniqueTransitionEvent not createTransitionEvent:** HIGHEST_PRIORITY so the picker runs before other resolve-side queue noise (01010).

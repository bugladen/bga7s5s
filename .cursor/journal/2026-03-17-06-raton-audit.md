# Ratón (_01096) Audit

## What I found

Three bugs, all fixed:

1. **Copy-paste name error** in `State_highDramaPhase01096.php` — said "Domenica" instead of "Ratón" in the `descriptionMyTurn`. Trivial fix.

2. **Array vs int comparison** in `Technique_01096.php` line 76 — `$adversary->Attachments > 0`. In PHP 8.x, `array > int` is always `true`. This means the technique would try to steal even when the adversary has no attachments. Changed to `count(...)`.

3. **Wound tracking scope bug** — the `AdversaryWoundedThisRound` flag was set from the moment the technique activated (during Ratón's round). If the adversary got wounded during Ratón's combat round, the flag carried over to the adversary's round check, incorrectly blocking the steal. The card says "during it" (the adversary's round), not "after this technique activates." Fixed by resetting the flag at the end of any non-adversary round, following the same pattern Panzerhand's technique uses for its `NoTechniquesThisRound` flag.

## WHY decisions

The wound-tracking fix uses an `else` branch in the `EventDuelEndOfRound` handler rather than scoping the `EventCharacterWounded` handler. Reason: it's simpler and more robust. Trying to filter wounds by "is this the adversary's round" inside the wound handler would require checking who the current round actor is, which may not be reliable during mid-round event processing. Resetting at round boundaries is clean and follows established patterns in the codebase (see Panzerhand journal entry).

## Things I verified are correct

- City Action correctly targets adjacent enemy characters with attachments, moves Ratón without engaging
- Technique correctly checks IN_DUEL, tracks adversary via getDuelRoundOpponent(), presents attachment list at end of adversary's round
- The "(no adversaries if challenge refused)" parenthetical is a rules reminder handled implicitly by the IN_DUEL check
- JS handlers, state wiring, and state constants are all correct
- The `actFromTechniqueWithId` properly unequips from adversary and equips to Ratón using `getRequiredAttachTargetId` for attachments that may redirect

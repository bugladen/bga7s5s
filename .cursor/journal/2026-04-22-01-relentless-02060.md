# Relentless (02060) Implementation

## Context
Continuing Tooth and Claw card implementations. Previous session did Quick Reflexes (02059).

## Card Text
**Reaction:** When your challenge is refused, wound your challenger. Wound the refusing character.
**Reaction:** When the duel would end, wound your participant. Wound your opposing adversary.

Neutral Risk card with Hubris trait. Two reactions, both wound two characters (your own + opponent's).

## Implementation

### Two Reactions, Mutually Exclusive Scenarios
The card has two reactions (a/b pattern, like _02030):
- **Reaction_02060a**: Triggers on `EventChallengeRejected`. If the card owner's player issued the challenge that was refused, offer to wound both the challenger (your character) and the refusing character.
- **Reaction_02060b**: Triggers on `EventDuelEnd`. If the card owner has a participant in the duel, offer to wound both your participant and the opposing adversary.

These are naturally mutually exclusive: if a challenge is refused, no duel happens. If a duel ends, the challenge was accepted. So sharing the same card's `isAvailable()` state is fine.

### Pattern Followed
Standard RiskReaction pay-from-hand pattern:
1. `handleEvent` detects trigger, saves character IDs, queues reaction transition
2. `performReaction` with 'woundBoth' queues pay state events
3. `EventRiskReactionTriggered` handler queues two wound events + notification + setUsed

### Key Decisions

**Parsing the bullet separator**: The card text uses "wound your challenger . Wound the refusing character" (separated by bullet). This means both wounds are effects that happen together - the card is thematic (Hubris: relentless aggression hurts you too).

**EventDuelEnd participant lookup**: Used `$event->challengingPlayerId`/`$event->defendingPlayerId` to determine which side belongs to the card owner, then saved the corresponding character IDs. No need for pre-event saving (unlike Reaction_01080 which saves during EventCharacterDestroyed before duel end).

**EventPlayerTurnEnd cleanup**: Both reactions reset their saved character IDs on EventPlayerTurnEnd, following the pattern from Reaction_02058 and Reaction_01080. This handles the case where the player passes on the reaction.

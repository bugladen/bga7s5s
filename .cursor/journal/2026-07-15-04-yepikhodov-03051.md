# Yepikhodov Yepikvidich (_03051) — create-character

## Card text
1. **City Action:** Move Yepikhodov to your Leader's location. Then, en garde your attachment there.
2. Your other characters at this location gain: **Technique:** Engage Yepikhodov's attachment • Copy the effects of a Technique on that attachment.

## Context
Continuing FAF after Daichi `_03050`. Scaffold is bare Character with Text already filled. Eddie pointed at `_01067` for grant/remove techniques to allies at location, and `Technique_02055` for copy-technique resolve.

## Approach / WHY

### Grant aura — Jean `_01067` shape, no trait filter
Text is "Your other characters" (not Musketeers). Same Recruited / Destroyed / CardMoved lifecycle as Jean/Stranahan, but grant `Technique_03051` to every other controlled character at Yepik's non-Home location. Yepik himself does **not** get the technique (he is the attachment source, not a recipient).

### City Action — move then engarde Engaged attachment
- Gate: `cardInCity`, Leader exists, Yepik ≠ Leader location, ≥1 Engaged non-Fake attachment that will be at the destination (Yepik's own engaged attachments travel with him + engaged attachments already on your characters at Leader's location).
- WHY require Engaged: En Garde on an already-ready attachment is a no-op; the printed "Then engarde" only does work if something is spent. Also creates synergy with the Technique's Engage cost (refresh then re-spend).
- Move with `engage=false` (Engage not printed). Then attachment button picker (`03038b_2` shape) — not board highlight.

### Technique_03051 — engage Yepik attachment + copy like Dame `02055`
- Owner of granted technique = the ally using it (actor must be owner).
- Source techniques come from Yepikhodov's **unengaged** attachments at the same location.
- WHY **not** filter source techniques via `isAvailableToPlayer`: those techniques check actor==Yepik; the duel actor is the ally, so availability would always fail. List all non-temporary techniques on unengaged Yepik attachments (exclude ClassId Technique_03051). Copy+activate+resolve+calc as 02055; engage the technique's owning attachment as the cost before resolve.

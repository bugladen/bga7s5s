## WHY decisions

### LTSSD cancel (01146b)
Pinned journal 2026-10-07-03: cancel reads `CHOSEN_TECHNIQUE_IS_MAIN` *before* delete and passes it into `EventTechniqueCanceled::$countsAsMainTechnique`. Opponent cancel of a main Technique still consumes the Technique opportunity (effects canceled ≠ opportunity refunded). Supersedes 2026-09-13-11 "technique_is_main stays 0". Also pinned duel-only gate (Discord/rules team comment in production).

### FakeGame harness
- `CHOSEN_MANEUVER`, `LETS_HAGGLE_EQUIP_TYPE`, `THEAH_ID` — Action_01147 / Reaction_01146b need these constants.
- `topCityCards` + `getCardsOnTopOfCityDeck` — _01149 When Revealed peeks deck without BGA Deck.
- `getPlayerColorById` — _01150 interveneList UI payload.
- `loadPlayersBasicInfos` now includes `player_id` key. WHY: _01150 compares `$player['player_id']` to ControllerId; without the key every player looked like an opponent (undefined != controller) and we got 2 transitions + PHP warnings.

### Bare nextState()
Action_01148 discard loop / finish and Card_01150 pass call `nextState()` with no arg. FakeGamestate records `null` (not `''`). `nextState("")` still records `''`. Pin that distinction — don't "fix" FakeGamestate to coerce null→''.

### Action_01149 availability
CityEventCard implements ICityDeckCard, so events increment both cityCards and eventCards tallies. "Only events" → equal counts → available. Non-event city card → city > event → unavailable. Pin that arithmetic; don't "simplify" to instanceof CityEventCard alone.

### Action_01147
createActionResolvedEvent intentionally omitted — hands off to equip pay. Test asserts LETS_HAGGLE_EQUIP_TYPE + attachmentSelected instead.

# Rapsodia (_04039)

## Classification
Printed:
1. While your performer is a Zealot, Academic, or Bard, this card has -1 cost.
2. City Action: Engage your performer and lose control of a City location • Claim your performer's location.

- Clause 1 = Pattern E.2 (Action-only performer-trait discount). No Maneuver printed → `getActionFromHandDiscount` on Action, do NOT invent a Maneuver. Mirror `_04018`.
- Clause 2 = Pattern A RiskCityAction + engage cost + location chooser for lose-control cost + claim effect. No printed "Target" → no Cesca.

## Approach
- Location chooser for which controlled City location to unclaim (mirror `Action_04030` JS trio / `Action_04cd04` unclaim).
- Engage + unclaim + claim all on location confirm (not at announce) so zombie/abort does not leave performer engaged without payoff. Contrast `_03034` engage-at-announce before character chooser.
- Grey when: no unengaged city performer at claimable location, OR player controls no City location that `canLocationBecomeUncontrolledBy`.
- Pass current controller as playerId on `createLocationBecomesUncontrolledEvent` (mirror `Action_01112a` / `Action_01130`).
- Add missing Trait `Performance` to TraitNames.

## Open questions / traps
- Allow losing control of the same location you are about to claim (if you already control it)? Text says "a City location" not "another" — allow. Net engage to re-claim own location is legal but pointless.
- Sole payoff is Claim → gate performers on `canLocationBeClaimedBy` like `Action_03053` / `Action_01103a`.

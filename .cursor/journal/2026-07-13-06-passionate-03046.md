# Passionate (_03046) — create-risk

## Card text
1. Duelist Reaction: After your performer intervenes • En garde them
2. Pirate Reaction: After your performer's challenge is accepted, if their adversary intervened • En garde your performer

## Context
Continuing FAF Castille after Curious (_03045). Two RiskReactions only — no Action/Maneuver/states/JS.

## Approach / WHY

### Two separate Reaction classes (a/b)
Mirror `_03027` / `_03016` split. Distinct trait gates + distinct engarde targets; one shared class with a mode field would be messier for `isAvailable`/`setUsed` and button copy.

### Both trigger on `EventCharacterIntervened` (not `EventChallengeAccepted`)
WHY: `actHighDramaChallengeActionIntervene` sets `CHALLENGE_ACCEPTED = true` but does **not** fire `EventChallengeAccepted` — only `EventCharacterIntervened` (+ usually engage). Pirate's "challenge accepted if adversary intervened" is exactly that path. Plain accept without intervene must not fire Pirate.

### Pattern D.2 (pay → effect in `EventRiskReactionTriggered`)
Same discipline as Subtle (`Reaction_03012`): defer engarde until after pay so cancel-during-pay can't leave a dangling engarde. Not D.3 — nothing to cancel ahead of.

### Trait gates on trigger-named performer (skill §)
- Duelist: intervener (`newTargetId`) is the performer → `hasTrait("Duelist")`
- Pirate: challenger (`CHOSEN_PERFORMER`) is "your performer" → `hasTrait("Pirate")`
Do NOT search the board for any Duelist/Pirate.

### Engarde timing vs engage queue
Intervene queues: Intervened, then Engaged. Reaction transition queues after both → by offer/pay time the intervener is Engaged (except Odette/Rena deferrals). At resolve, only `createCardEngardedEvent` if still `Engaged`.

### No `IRiskThatTargetsCharacters`
Fixed target from the trigger — no character chooser.

### No new states/JS
Framework reaction + pay states only.

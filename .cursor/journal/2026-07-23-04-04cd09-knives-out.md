# _04cd09 Knives Out (bas City Event)

## Clarification
Eddie said `_04cd07` but that card is already fully implemented (pure dual-Forced; skill exemplar). Focused file + "Unlimited means no setUsed" match `_04cd09` Knives Out. Implemented 04cd09.

## Card Text
1. Passive: Characters at this location cannot refuse challenges.
2. City Action: Engage your performer OR discard a card → Move this card to another City location. Unlimited.

## Classification
1. Location-scoped refuse lock → Daichi `_03050` pattern (static helper + ArgumentsTrait + FrameworkActionsTrait + JS disable), NOT a new CHALLENGE_TYPE. WHY: applies to ANY challenge at the location, not challenges issued by this card.
2. City Action → EventCityAction + multi-step states. Unlimited → call `setUsed(false)` after central confirm marks Used (Siren's Scream / Crabs pattern) but NO `playersUsed` — truly reusable by anyone any number of times.

## WHY choices
- **Daichi gate over CHALLENGE_TYPE** for the passive: Knives Out isn't issuing challenges; it locks refuse for whoever is challenged *at its location*. Same channel as Daichi (`cannotRefuseDueToKnivesOut` arg + server throw).
- **Three states + EVENTS hop after cost**: pay engage/discard by queueing events, then `createTransitionEvent("04cd09_3")` and `nextState("costPaid")` → EVENTS. WHY: going straight to 04cd09_3 would leave engage/discard queued until later; discard UX also needs the hand discard to actually process before location pick.
- **RequiresPerformerSelected = false**: discard path needs no performer; engage picks in step 2.
- **Numeric cost ids 1/2**: `actFromCardWithId` is int-typed.
- **Destination = any other city location**: printed "another City location", not adjacent.
- **setUsed(false) only** (no playersUsed): Eddie clarified Unlimited = do not call setUsed (meaning don't leave Used stuck). Central confirm still sets Used=true, so we clear it — same defensive call as Siren's Scream, without per-player day tracking.
- **No discard of the event card**: action moves it; card stays in play.

## Decisions

**SchemeCityAction not SchemeAction** — printed keyword is "Action" not "City Action", but "your performer issues a challenge to target opposing" needs a city performer. Same base as `_03042` / Unsanctioned `_02061`. En Garde is the status gate (`!$Engaged`), Duelist is the trait gate. Not ISorcererAbility.

**En Garde ≠ Engage** — no "Engage your performer" printed. Do NOT engage in ActionTriggered or stSetupChallenge. Custom `STAND_YOUR_GROUND_CHALLENGE_TYPE` kept **off** `stIssueChallenge` auto-engage list **and** off Unsanctioned's stSetupChallenge special-case engage. Sanjay/When Least Expected trichotomy case (c): never engages.

**Unrefusable** — copy Unsanctioned/Epee: FrameworkActionsTrait throw + JS disable Refuse. Do **not** gate intervene (reminder text: intervening accepts). Zombie on accept-challenge currently defaults to Reject — must Accept for this type or zombie throws.

**No HD GameState** — transition `"04045"` → `HIGH_DRAMA_CHALLENGE_ACTION_CHOOSE_TARGET` like `_03042` / `_02061`. Framework target picker + `IAbilityThatTargetsCharacters`.

**Threat on accept** — `EventGenerateChallengeThreat` `actorThreat += 1` (Sworn Swords `_03030`). Actor is the challenger = "your participant". Fires after intervene too (accept).

**Adversary destroyed / accept-time threat** — Issuer = this scheme's owner controls the **challenger** of a `STAND_YOUR_GROUND` duel. Destroyed payoff is the **defender** only. Do **not** stamp `$IssuedThisChallenge` on the Action: `EventActionResolved` runs with `!IN_DUEL` during the challenge pipeline (before generate-threat / duel) and wiped the flag — accept-time +1 actor threat disappeared. `CHALLENGE_TYPE` + challenger controller is enough for a mirror match without persistence.

**Pre-commit** — challenge-flow `createActionResolvedEvent()` comment like Cornered / `_03042`.

# Horizontal faction flags from Metadata Title

## Task
User asked for 5 new images derived from `misc/Assets/metadata/7s5s Metadata Title.png` — original has 5 vertical pennants on a horizontal pole; wanted each as a horizontal flag on a vertical pole.

## What I did
Generated 5 images via GenerateImage with the title PNG as reference. Copied into `misc/Assets/metadata/`:
- `flag-horizontal-sun.jpg` (light blue + gold sun face)
- `flag-horizontal-lion.jpg` (green + white rampant lion)
- `flag-horizontal-dragon.jpg` (dark grey + dragon)
- `flag-horizontal-hand.jpg` (red + yellow open hand)
- `flag-horizontal-wreath.jpg` (purple + gold wreath)

Also live under Cursor assets as `flag-castille-sun.jpg` etc. (faction names guessed from emblems — may not match CoFS naming; filename in metadata folder is emblem-based).

## WHY
Kept black background + wooden pole + gold finial to match title art language. Horizontal pennant (point right) off vertical pole is the geometric flip the user asked for.

## Notes
AI gen won't pixel-match original emblems. Dragon came out more illustrative than the original silhouette; hand flag got a darker tip. User may want regenerations or Photoshop cleanup if they need exact heraldry.

# A Shared Interest `_04051`

## Orientation

Scaffold already correct vs art: Name, Init 31, Panache -1, Bargain/Savvy, Neutral.

Text clauses:
1. Resolve: Choose opponent → they add Renown to any city loc → you add Renown to a **different** city loc. Pattern A multi-state (closest sibling `_02025` Tea and Cakes, but inverted order + "different" constraint).
2. Forced at Planning End: you claim a City loc → chosen player claims a **different** City loc. Pattern F. "the chosen player" = opponent persisted from resolve → must live on the scheme (`$chosenOpponentId`), not `Game::CHOSEN_OPPONENT` (transient across phases).

## WHY persist on scheme

`_02025` only needs the opponent for the next resolve state, so globals are fine. Forced fires later in the same day after draw/etc., so the pick must survive serialization on the card until Planning End (and clear after Forced / on locker).

## Design

Resolve states:
- `04051` — controller picks opponent (buttons)
- `04051_2` — opponent picks any city Renown
- `04051_3` — controller picks different city Renown (`locationIds` exclude `$firstRenownLocation`)

Planning End:
- Gate `EventPhasePlanningEnd` + `LOCATION_PLAYER_HOME` + `$chosenOpponentId > 0`
- Skip Forced (notify) if no claimable city locs via `canLocationBeClaimedBy`
- `04051` — controller claims
- `04051_2` — opponent claims different; skip if none left claimable ≠ first claim

Claim with `createLocationClaimedEvent($playerId, null, $location)` — same null-performer as `Reaction_03005`.

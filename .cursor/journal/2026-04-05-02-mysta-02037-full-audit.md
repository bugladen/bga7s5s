## Mysta _02037 — Full Card Text Audit

Audited all three abilities on Mysta against card text. Everything is correct.

### Card Text
1. **Forced**: When this card would be put in the discard pile, sink it instead.
2. **Technique**: The adversary cannot gamble during their next round.
3. **Reaction**: After an opposing character issues a challenge • Equip this card to your character at this location.

### Forced — Correct
`_02037::handleEvent` intercepts both `EventCardDiscardedFromHand` and `EventCardDiscardedFromPlay`, cancels the event, and queues `createCardAddedToFactionDeckEvent(..., onTop: false)`. The `onTop = false` path in EventHub explicitly says "sunk to the bottom." This covers all ways a card can be "put in the discard pile."

### Technique — Correct
`Technique_02037` sets a gamble lock on `EventResolveTechnique`, blocks via `eventCheck` throwing on `EventDuelAttemptGamble`, and clears on the owning character's next `EventDuelNewRound` (i.e., after the adversary's round). This timing is identical to the pattern in `Technique_01186` (Maryam's maneuver block). Exception type bug was already fixed in earlier session today (see `2026-04-05-01`).

### Reaction — Correct
`Reaction_02037` triggers on `EventChallengeIssued` from an opposing character. Mysta must be in hand. Finds eligible characters at the challenger's location owned by Mysta's controller. `performReaction` validates target, equips via `createAttachmentEquippedEvent`, marks reaction used. Pass option cleans up without marking used so it can re-trigger.

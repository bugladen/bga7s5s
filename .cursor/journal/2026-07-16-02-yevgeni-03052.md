## WHY decisions
- **Continuous Reaction** for unlabelled "At the beginning of Dusk, you may…" (same as Angeline/Ekaterina After…may). Dusk begin is once/day so Continuous ≈ daily; Continuous matches the phrasing and omits `setUsed(true)` (literal kept in comment for pre-commit).
- **Reaction → transition states** (01144 shape) because sink+reorder needs chooseList; button Reaction alone can't host that UI.
- **Sink ≠ discard**: `createCardAddedToCityDeckEvent(..., onTop=false)` like Kaspar 01035. 02014 discards to city discard — wrong keyword.
- **Private args** (`argsForStatePrivate`) for city peek (Penya 01177_2) and hand look. Technique_03043 *reveals* publicly with multiplayer Ok — wrong for "Look at".
- **Auto-finish reorder** when ≤1 card remains after sink — order is forced; skip useless sort UI.
- **Gambling Technique** gates: `IN_DUEL` + `DUEL_GAMBLED` + actor identity; nonempty hand so the technique isn't a no-op.
- **Not ISorcererAbility** — abilities aren't printed as Sorcerer abilities despite Sorcerer trait.

## Transition key

## WHY decisions

### Reaction Continuous + opponent-effect gate
Printed as "After … they may en garde" with no Reaction keyword → Continuous like Ekaterina claim-move (no `setUsed(true)`; literal kept in comment for pre-commit).

"Opponent's effect" ≠ Challenge auto-engage (`sourceId == 0` in FrameworkActionsTrait). Mirror `Reaction_03031::isOpponentAbility`: source card ControllerId is a live opponent, or in-play action owner's controller. Own engages / auto-engage do not prompt.

"Your other Musketeer at this location": same controller, Musketeer trait, `Location == Aimée.Location`, `Id != Aimée`. Home shared-location false positives filtered by ControllerId.

Stash `engagedMusketeerId` public for serialize. En Garde via `createCardEngardedEvent`. Re-validate Engaged in performReaction — EventCardEngaged has `runEventHubAfterCards=true` so Engaged may still be false during handleEvent; by button click hub has applied.

### Copy Technique (04021a)
Closest sibling is Yepikhodov `Technique_03051` but sources are **character** techniques on other Musketeers, not attachments. Parenthetical forbids attachments — list only `$character->getTechniques()`, and reject `getOwningAttachment() !== null` on pick.

Skip `isAvailableToPlayer` on sources (same WHY as 03051): those techniques gate actor==Musketeer; duel actor is Aimée.

No Engage cost (text has none). Dame clone/activate/resolve/calc pipeline. Transition key `"04021"` → `DUEL_CHOOSE_TECHNIQUE_04021` (52104021). JS buttons mirror 03051 in `OnUpdateActionButtons.bas.js` only (no OnEntering — button list).

### En Garde +1 Thrust (04021b)
Extend `Technique_PlusOneThrust` (reuse duel calc + challenge threat). En Garde = `!$owner->Engaged` precondition only — do not engage as cost. During IN_DUEL also require actor==owner (03025a shape). Outside duel leave PlusOneThrust challenge path open when En Garde.

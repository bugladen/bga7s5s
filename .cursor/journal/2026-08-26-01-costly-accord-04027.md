## Text → pattern
`<b>En Garde Diplomat Action:</b> Target an opposing non-<b>Leader</b> that is en garde • They may engage. If they do not, claim this location.`

- Plain **Action** (not City) → Pattern B `RiskAction`; performers from `parent::getPerformersForAction` (home eligible in principle).
- **En Garde** heading → `!$Engaged` precondition on performer, **not** Engage cost.
- **Diplomat** → mechanical `hasTrait("Diplomat")` gate — not Sorcerer.
- Printed **Target** character → Cesca: `IAbilityThatTargetsCharacters` + Risk `IRiskThatTargetsCharacters`.
- Target filters: opposing (same location), `!Leader`, en garde (`!$Engaged`).
- Effect: opponent may engage; decline → you claim location. Mirror Yield `_02020_3` / Duckfoot `_01049_2` Engage vs Decline buttons (not Pass — consequence is claim, label it). Claim gate with `canLocationBeClaimedBy` at emit only (Censure `_03057` discipline — do **not** grey Action when unclaimable).

## WHY choices
- **Engage vs Decline buttons** (Yield `_02020_3`) over Pass (`_01034`): claim is a concrete opponent-facing consequence — label it.

- **Claimability only at decline emit**: Action stays available when location can't be claimed (they might still engage). Same as Censure.
